## LinkedIn Post

Most teams report cost per lead. They don't report the 39-hour gap between when the lead is created and when sales first touches it.

LXA and LeanData's 2026 B2B State of Martech & Revenue Operations survey (201 enterprise organizations) puts the operational reality on record: 73% have no documented SLA between marketing and sales, 61% route leads manually, and only 11% have applied AI to the routing layer. (lxahub.com/resource-b2b-state-of-martech-rev-ops)

The 39-hour figure matters because leads contacted within five minutes convert at 2.6 times the rate of those reached after 24 hours. Most enterprise teams are operating at 78 times slower than the conversion-optimal threshold.

Pipeline reviews surface this as a conversion rate problem. The root usually sits in routing.

AI investment in B2B revenue teams concentrates on scoring models and campaign optimization — the signal side of the process. The mechanical step that determines whether any signal actually reaches a salesperson is largely untouched. Process and Operations has been the lowest-scoring pillar in B2B RevOps maturity measurement for three consecutive years.

What gets reported is the output. What doesn't get reported is whether the handoff worked.

## Central Insight
Most B2B demand gen programs are measured from MQL creation forward, but the operational mechanics between MQL creation and first sales touch are failing at scale. The average enterprise takes 39 hours to contact a new MQL — 78x slower than the 5-minute window that produces 2.6x higher conversion — and 73% of B2B organizations have no documented marketing-sales SLA. Meanwhile, only 11% have applied AI to the routing layer despite applying it upstream in scoring and content.

## Why This Matters
- The gap between demand gen output (MQLs, pipeline influenced) and pipeline conversion is widely attributed to campaign quality or market conditions — but the mechanical handoff layer is the more frequent root cause, and it is rarely surfaced in pipeline reviews
- Only 11% of enterprise B2B organizations have AI in lead routing despite investing heavily in AI for content and scoring — the application layer closest to pipeline is being left behind
- Process and Operations has been the lowest-scoring pillar in B2B RevOps maturity research for three consecutive years, suggesting this is structural, not solvable by adding budget or tools

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- Foundation Inc and AirOps analyzed 57.2 million AI citations across 50 B2B brands and 7 verticals over 60 days. Only 10.15% of LLM citations link to brand-owned content. In 2 out of 3 AI responses, the brand's own content is completely absent even when the brand is being discussed. When buyers search by brand name, own content appears in 77.6% of responses — but category and problem-level queries produce near-zero brand-owned citations.
Source: Foundation Inc — https://foundationinc.co/lab/foundation-marketing-airops-report

- AirOps citation tracking: Only 30% of brands remain consistently visible from one AI response to the next for the same query. Only 20% maintain visibility across five consecutive runs. 85% of top-of-funnel AI brand visibility comes from unowned domains — review sites, Reddit, YouTube, community forums, competitor pages.
Source: AirOps — https://www.airops.com/blog/llm-brand-citation-tracking
