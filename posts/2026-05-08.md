## LinkedIn Post

DerivateX published a benchmark study analyzing 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini — 1,400 buyer-intent prompts, scored on a composite AI presence scale.

The average score was 56.9 out of 100. 44% of companies scored below 50.

What stands out is the spread: the highest scorer reached 89, the lowest scored 2 — both operating in established software categories with active marketing teams.

That 87-point gap doesn't map cleanly to marketing budget or team size. It suggests something more structural: traditional marketing investment isn't translating to AI visibility.

This has a compounding effect because 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools during research, and the share of B2B tech queries triggering an AI Overview grew from 36% to 82% in twelve months. AI-assisted research is already a first-touch channel for a majority of buyers.

The platform fragmentation adds another layer. Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. ChatGPT favors encyclopedic content, Perplexity heavily cites Reddit, Google AI Overviews prefer YouTube and multi-modal material. A content strategy optimized for one platform doesn't carry to the others.

The pattern this suggests: most B2B marketing teams are running a single-input visibility strategy in an environment that now distributes discovery across citation systems with entirely different logic.

Full benchmark: https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/derivatex-study-finds-b2b-saas-companies-are-invisible-to-ai-assisted-buyers/52463/

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- DerivateX benchmarked 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini using 1,400 buyer-intent prompts. Average AI Presence Score: 56.9/100. 44% of companies scored below 50. The gap between highest scorer (89) and lowest (2) is 87 points — both companies had active marketing teams in established categories. Implication: traditional marketing investment is not creating AI visibility.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/derivatex-study-finds-b2b-saas-companies-are-invisible-to-ai-assisted-buyers/52463/

- 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity) in their research process. Share of B2B tech queries triggering an AI Overview grew from 36% to 82% in 12 months. AI-assisted research is already a first-touch channel, not a future state.
Source: Superlines — https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/

- Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. ChatGPT favors encyclopedic content (47.9% of top citations), Perplexity heavily cites Reddit (46.7%), Google AI Overviews prefer YouTube/multi-modal (23.3%). Each platform has distinct citation logic — a single-channel content strategy doesn't transfer.
Source: averi.ai — https://www.averi.ai/how-to/chatgpt-vs.-perplexity-vs.-google-ai-mode-the-b2b-saas-citation-benchmarks-report-(2026)

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn ROAS reached 121% in 2025, outperforming Google Search (67%) and Meta (51%). Cost per company influenced dropped from €154 to €70.11 YoY. But CPC rose from $6.20 to $6.50 and CPM from $37.90 to $39.20 — rising competition is squeezing unit-level efficiency even as platform ROAS improves.
Source: Dreamdata — https://dreamdata.io/blog/announcing-linkedin-ads-benchmarks-report-2026

- LinkedIn now influences 28.3% of new business deals (up from 15% prior year). Average B2B customer journey lengthened to 272 days from 211 days — buyers entering pipeline later but with higher intent.
Source: Dreamdata — https://dreamdata.io/linkedin-ads-b2b-benchmarks

- Google's February/March 2026 Core Updates targeted scaled AI content and parasitic SEO. Sites publishing original research gained ~22% visibility on average; sites relying on generic AI-generated content without editorial oversight saw 60–80% traffic drops.
Source: Amsive — https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/google-march-2026-core-update-winners-losers-analysis/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- 87% of organizations report their marketing investments produce unreliable or inflated intent signals; only 26% of those signals convert into qualified opportunities. Root cause: most intent platforms tap the same co-op data sources — competitors see the same signals simultaneously, eroding any differentiation.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/the-intent-data-playbook-is-breaking-down/

- ABM-led programs generate 2.6x more pipeline per marketing dollar vs. broad-reach demand gen. But only 13% of MQLs convert to SQLs at most B2B SaaS companies — 87% of spend never reaches a sales conversation.
Source: digitalapplied.com — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/abm-account-based-marketing-statistics-2026

- B2B pipeline forecasting accuracy improved from 54% (2024) to 71% (2026), driven by intent data, AI-assisted scoring, and tighter MQL-to-SQL definitions.
Source: digitalapplied.com — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/b2b-marketing-statistics-2026-essential-data-points

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- AI Overviews now appear in 82% of B2B tech searches (up from 36% 12 months ago). The #1 organic ranking page sees a 34.5% CTR drop when an AI Overview is present. Organic CTR drops 61% overall for AI Overview queries.
Source: dataslayer.ai — https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-the-end-of-traditional-ctr-and-how-to-adapt-in-2025

- Content with statistics, citations, and quotations achieves 30–40% higher AI response visibility. Pages updated within 2 months earn 28% more citations than older content.
Source: Superlines — https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- CFOs are taking increased control of GTM strategy, compressing marketing autonomy in budget and tool selection. Teams with mature attribution (the "capable cohort") spend 23% more on martech but report 1.6x larger marketing-sourced pipeline — attribution maturity is becoming a pipeline growth differentiator.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/why-cfos-are-taking-control-of-gtm-strategy/

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Cybersecurity is the fastest-growing MSP segment, expanding at 18% annually — outpacing the overall managed services market at 14%. 60% of buyers cite cybersecurity as the top challenge that led them to engage an MSP.
Source: Huntress — https://www.huntress.com/msp-guide/msp-security-industry-trends

- MSP cybersecurity buyers are shifting away from point-solution evaluation toward AI-driven resilience narratives. Buyers now assume AI-powered threats will accelerate attack timelines; MSP marketing that leads with tool specs rather than business continuity outcomes is underperforming.
Source: Channel Insider — https://www.channelinsider.com/security/managed-services/channel-trends-2026-ai-msp/
