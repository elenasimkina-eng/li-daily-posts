## LinkedIn Post

Most teams share the cost-per-meeting improvement from AI SDRs. Almost none share what happens two months later.

The benchmark numbers look strong: AI SDR programs are cutting cost-per-meeting from roughly $312 to $94, a 70% reduction that shows up immediately in the marketing dashboard and gets cited in quarterly reviews.

What doesn't get shared: in programs running AI SDRs without human handoff, meeting-to-opportunity conversion drops 41%. The cost savings are real. The pipeline quality gap shows up in the SQL stage, tracked by a different team, 60-90 days later.

This creates a measurement gap by design. Marketing owns the input metric. Sales owns the output metric. In most B2B orgs, those reports don't sit next to each other, and by the time the conversion shortfall surfaces, the quarterly AI SDR ROI slides have already been approved.

The number worth tracking is pipeline-per-SDR-dollar, not cost-per-meeting. But that requires connecting a marketing cost center to a sales outcome across two systems, and most teams haven't built that bridge.

41% of enterprise B2B teams now run at least one AI SDR in production, up from 3% in early 2024. The adoption curve is steep. The measurement infrastructure is not.

Source: DevCommX 2026 AI SDR Statistics — https://www.devcommx.com/blogs/ai-sdr-statistics; Brilo AI SDR Benchmark — https://www.brilo.ai/resources/ai-sdr-tools-outbound-automation-trends
