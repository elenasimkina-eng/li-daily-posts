## LinkedIn Post

The standard ABM playbook says personalize everything. The last 12 months of data suggest that's the wrong level of analysis.

Gartner surveyed 632 B2B buyers and found that content focused on individual-level relevance has a 59% negative impact on buying group consensus. Content designed for buying group relevance, by contrast, improves consensus by 20%. Buyers exposed to buying group-level content are 3x more likely to report a high-quality deal.

This matters because "no decision" is now the default outcome in B2B. Across 478 companies tracked through 2025, 83–84% of opportunities end without a purchase. GTM effectiveness has fallen from 78% in 2018 to 47% today. Median MQL-to-SQL conversion dropped from 13.1% to 9.8% in a single year.

Meanwhile, the top use case teams report for AI in ABM is content personalization at scale — 29% of programs, per the Demand Gen Report's 2026 benchmark. Teams are scaling the thing that fragments buying groups, at the moment buying group fragmentation is the dominant reason deals stall.

The assumption buried in most personalization programs is that reaching each stakeholder with individually relevant content will move them forward. What it may actually be doing is reinforcing each person's separate framing of the problem, making consensus harder to reach, not easier.

Personalization isn't wrong. The unit of personalization is.
