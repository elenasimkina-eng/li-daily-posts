## LinkedIn Post

LinkedIn just rewired its feed algorithm. The change is more structural than most LinkedIn strategy posts are treating it.

The new LLM-powered ranking system, rolled out in late May/early June 2026, penalizes the formats B2B demand gen teams spent years optimizing: carousel posts, external links, promoted reach plays. Native text posts now average 28% higher reach and 34% better engagement than carousels published in the same period, per Markana Media's June 2026 analysis (markanamedia.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm-june-2026-b2b-reach/).

But the more interesting signal is what this change reveals about where LinkedIn now sits in the buyer journey.

73% of B2B buyers use AI tools to research vendors first. They build an initial consideration set in ChatGPT or Perplexity, then turn to LinkedIn and peer networks to validate it. AI narrows the shortlist. LinkedIn closes the credibility gap.

Thought Leader Ads — personal, specific, discussion-generating — are delivering 3x higher CTR and 40–50% lower CPL than standard sponsored content (ALM Corp, almcorp.com/blog/linkedin-feed-algorithm-update-llm-2026/). That's the content format that functions in a validation context, not a discovery one.

LinkedIn's algorithm is now rewarding content designed for the stage buyers actually use the platform for. Most B2B LinkedIn strategies were not.

That's the misalignment. Reach was never the platform's job in an AI-shaped buying environment.
