## LinkedIn Post

B2B buyers spend the first 220 days forming purchase opinions before entering any vendor's sales pipeline.

Dreamdata's 2026 benchmarks report — drawn from 66 million sessions and 3.5 million customer journeys — shows the full B2B buying cycle now runs 272 days, spans 88 touchpoints, and involves 10 stakeholders. The first 80% of that journey, roughly seven months, happens entirely outside sales conversations and CRM records.

Most demand generation infrastructure is built to measure the last 52 days.

That gap isn't a minor attribution inconvenience. It means teams are running sprint-based campaign evaluations against a buying journey that operates on a nine-month clock. Campaigns that appear to underperform inside a 30-day window may be doing the foundational work — category education, vendor shortlisting, brand familiarity — that "high-performing" campaigns later collect credit for.

LinkedIn ROAS in the same dataset reaches 121%, against Google Search at 67% and Meta at 51%. That number likely reflects capture, not cause. The channels winning on pipeline-adjacent attribution are inheriting influence built months earlier.

This starts to look less like a media buying problem and more like a measurement architecture problem.

The question isn't which channels drive pipeline. It's which channels are operating in the 220-day window where the decision actually forms — and whether any current reporting framework can see it.

Source: Dreamdata 2026 LinkedIn Ads B2B Benchmarks — https://dreamdata.io/blog/announcing-linkedin-ads-benchmarks-report-2026

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity) in purchase research. AI search traffic converts at 14.2% vs. Google organic 2.8%. Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity — the platforms draw from distinct source pools. Only 22% of marketers track AI visibility. The conversion advantage exists but is largely uncaptured because teams aren't measuring or building for the channel.
Source: Yahoo Finance / multi-source analysis — https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/73-b2b-buyers-ai-tools-231200431.html

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn's April 2026 algorithm overhaul (rebuilt with LLMs) deprioritizes company page content and surface-level commentary in favor of original insight and proprietary data. Sponsored content now occupies ~40% of the average LinkedIn feed. The distribution model now mirrors relevance-based ranking, the same pivot Instagram and TikTok made years earlier — meaning brand pages with promotional tone lose organic reach regardless of follower count.
Source: ALM Corp — https://almcorp.com/blog/linkedin-feed-algorithm-update-llm-2026/

**ABM & Demand Generation**

Critically: buyers spend the first 220 of those 272 days forming decisions before entering any vendor's sales pipeline. B2B marketers now own 81% of the full buyer journey. LinkedIn delivers 121% ROAS vs. Google Search at 67% and Meta at 51%. Cost per company influenced dropped from €154 to €70.11.
Source: Dreamdata 2026 LinkedIn Ads B2B Benchmarks — https://dreamdata.io/blog/announcing-linkedin-ads-benchmarks-report-2026

Brands cited inside an AI Overview earn 35% more clicks than non-cited competitors at standard position one — being cited is now more valuable than ranking below the overview.
