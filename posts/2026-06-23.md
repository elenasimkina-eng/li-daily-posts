## LinkedIn Post

DiNardi's point about the dark SEO funnel holds — but the fix most teams are drawing from it is off.

The argument: 68% of B2B buyers now start vendor discovery in AI tools before they reach Google. Traffic misrepresents what's actually driving pipeline. Citation is the real signal. That part is correct.

Where teams go wrong: they take that insight and invest in optimizing company page content for AI visibility.

Meltwater analyzed 9.5 million AI citations across 16 B2B categories. 75% of LinkedIn citations came from individual member profiles. Company pages: 25%. More striking — 51% of cited authors had fewer than 10,000 followers.

AI models aren't prioritizing well-resourced brand content. They're citing individual experts who write with specificity and data. The credential that earns a citation is the quality of the thinking, not the budget behind the channel.

The demand gen implication is uncomfortable: if your AI visibility strategy is centered on the company channel, you're building on the wrong asset. The citation layer runs through people, not pages.

Gaetano DiNardi surfaced the measurement failure clearly in his March 2026 LinkedIn article on the dark SEO funnel. The question is whether teams follow the evidence to where the solution actually lives.

Curious whether anyone tracking citation sources across personal and brand channels is seeing the same split.

Source: Meltwater LinkedIn AI Citation Study — https://www.meltwater.com/en/about/press-releases/how-ai-is-changing-the-way-information-is-found

## Central Insight
Gaetano DiNardi's "dark SEO funnel" claim — that AI tool discovery has overtaken Google for B2B buyers — is directionally correct, but the operational implication most teams are acting on (optimize company page content for AI visibility) is wrong. Meltwater's 9.5M citation analysis shows 75% of LinkedIn AI citations come from individual member profiles, not company pages, and 51% of cited authors have fewer than 10,000 followers. The citation layer runs through people, not brand infrastructure.

## Why This Matters
- Demand gen teams building AI visibility strategies around company page optimization are investing in the wrong asset
- The Meltwater data decouples citation authority from follower size and budget, which reshapes how headcount and content investment should be allocated
- This creates a structural tension for B2B brands: the individuals most likely to earn AI citations are often not under direct marketing control

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- LinkedIn is now the #2 most-cited source in AI answers, after YouTube. Meltwater analyzed 9.5 million AI citations across 16 B2B categories using its GenAI Lens. Key finding: 75% of LinkedIn citations came from individual member profiles vs. 25% from company pages. 51% of cited authors had fewer than 10,000 followers. Implication for demand gen: brand content budgets are largely funding the wrong channel for AI discovery.
Source: Meltwater — https://www.meltwater.com/en/about/press-releases/how-ai-is-changing-the-way-information-is-found

- DerivateX analyzed 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini using 1,400 buyer-intent prompts. Average AI Presence Score: 56.9 out of 100. 44% of companies scored below 50. The gap between highest scorer (Clio: 89) and lowest (LeadSquared: 2) is 87 points, despite both having active marketing teams in established categories.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/derivatex-study-finds-b2b-saas-companies-are-invisible-to-ai-assisted-buyers/52463/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google AI Overviews appear on roughly 1 in 5 Google searches and reduce organic traffic by approximately 15% on affected queries. However, brands cited inside AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than non-cited competitors on the same queries. Average organic CTR with AI Overviews drops from 3.97% (without) to 0.64% (with).
Source: AuthorityTech — https://authoritytech.io/blog/google-ai-overviews-impact-seo-2026

- LinkedIn's June 2026 algorithm update penalizes posts with external links by 60% in distribution. Thought Leader Ads (promoting individual profile posts through the company page) now deliver 3x higher CTR and 40–50% lower CPL compared to standard Sponsored Content.
Source: Digital Applied — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm-2026-engagement-strategy-guide
