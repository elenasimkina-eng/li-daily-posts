## LinkedIn Post

The standard playbook says intent data identifies who's in-market. Run more signals, prioritize more accounts, accelerate pipeline.

The last 60 days of benchmark data suggest the model has a structural problem.

Energize Marketing's 2026 Cybersecurity Demand Generation Benchmark found 99% of cybersecurity marketers use third-party intent data — and only 7% consider it "very effective." Separately, DemandScience surveyed 750 senior marketing leaders for their 2026 State of Performance Marketing Report: 87% report campaigns yielding unreliable or inflated intent signals, and only 26% of those signals convert into qualified opportunities.

The issue isn't the concept. Intent signals exist and they matter. The issue is market saturation of the same data. When every vendor in a category subscribes to the same third-party intent provider, the "in-market" trigger fires simultaneously across competing SDR teams. The account that spiked on "endpoint detection" last Tuesday has received eleven outreach sequences by Thursday. That's not targeting an in-market buyer. That's crowding one.

What holds up in the Energize Marketing cybersecurity data: research-driven content and executive-level insights continue to outperform lighter demand gen formats for generating qualified pipeline. The brands converting intent signals into conversations had already earned credibility before the spike.

Intent data tells you who's researching. It doesn't explain why they're not responding.

## Central Insight
When every competitor uses the same third-party intent data, the "competitive advantage" of knowing who's in-market disappears — you're all arriving simultaneously, chasing the same accounts with the same triggers, and the signal becomes market noise rather than a differentiator.

- 99% adoption with only 7% reporting "very effective" indicates the tool is commoditized, not broken — the strategic moat has closed
- 87% of organizations receiving inflated/unreliable signals means the pipeline math most demand gen teams are running is built on flawed inputs
- The actual performance driver per the cybersecurity benchmark is credibility and content quality established before the intent spike — which is the opposite of reactive signal-chasing
