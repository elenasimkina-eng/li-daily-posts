## LinkedIn Post

The assumption behind most martech budgets is that more coverage equals more pipeline.

The 2026 data doesn't support it.

Companies running 5 or fewer core tools generate 23% more marketing-attributed pipeline per headcount than teams running 25 or more — a gap that shows up in annual planning. (Topicintelligence.ai — https://topicintelligence.ai/martech-consolidation-2026/)

The market is confirming the same direction. Total martech products grew just 0.7% in 2026, with roughly 1,500 added and 1,300 removed. AI was expected to expand the stack; instead it's making point solutions redundant faster than new ones can replace them. (House of Martech — https://houseofmartech.com/blog/2026-martech-vendor-consolidation-wave-what-the-acquisitions-mean-for-your-stack-strategy)

22% of marketing budget goes to martech on average. 47% of operators say stack complexity is actively blocking value extraction from the tools already in place.

Consolidation is starting to look less like a cost-cutting move and more like a pipeline efficiency decision.
