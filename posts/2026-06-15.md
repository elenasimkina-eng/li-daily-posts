## LinkedIn Post

Nearly half of B2B SaaS companies are functionally invisible in the AI systems their buyers use to research purchases.

DerivateX's 2026 AI Visibility Benchmark tested 50 companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. 1,400 buyer-intent prompts. 44% scored below 50 out of 100. The spread between the highest scorer and lowest: 87 points.

This is happening while companies are building AI into their marketing and GTM workflows at pace. ICONIQ's 2026 State of Go-to-Market, drawn from 150+ B2B executives and portfolio operating data, found that high-AI-adoption teams run 43% fewer GTM FTEs at the same revenue band, with AE quota attainment 8 percentage points higher. The internal efficiency case for AI is real.

But internal efficiency and buyer-facing visibility aren't the same problem.

When 87% of B2B marketers have embedded generative AI in at least one workflow, and 44% are still absent from the shortlisting process buyers run in AI before entering anyone's funnel, the assumption that's breaking is this: AI adoption in marketing and AI presence for buyers are not the same investment.

One reduces headcount cost per dollar of pipeline. The other determines whether you appear when a buyer asks an AI which vendors solve this problem.

Most teams have a budget line for AI tools. Very few have a budget line for AI visibility.

Sources: DerivateX 2026 AI Visibility Benchmark — derivatex.agency/report/ai-visibility-b2b-saas-2026 | ICONIQ State of Go-to-Market 2026 — iconiq.com/growth/reports/state-of-go-to-market-2026

## Central Insight
AI adoption in marketing operations (efficiency, workflow automation, headcount leverage) and AI visibility for buyers (presence in AI-assisted research and shortlisting) are two separate strategic problems — and most B2B companies are only solving the first one.

## Why This Matters
- The assumption that investing in AI for marketing = being visible to AI-assisted buyers is quietly breaking, with measurable data now available on both sides
- Companies demonstrating real GTM efficiency gains from AI (ICONIQ) may be simultaneously losing ground in the pre-funnel buyer research stage (DerivateX)
- For demand gen directors, this creates two distinct and non-overlapping budget and strategy questions: AI for internal operations vs. AI presence as a distribution channel

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- DerivateX analyzed 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini using 1,400 buyer-intent prompts. Result: 44% of companies scored below 50/100 on AI visibility. Average AI Presence Score: 56.9/100. Highest scorer: Clio at 89; lowest: LeadSquared at 2. The gap spans 87 points. Implication: Nearly half of B2B SaaS vendors are functionally absent when AI-assisted buyers research purchase decisions — before any paid or owned channel is reached.
Source: DerivateX — https://derivatex.agency/report/ai-visibility-b2b-saas-2026/

- 87% of B2B marketers now use generative AI in at least one workflow (up from 51% two years ago), yet only 41% can demonstrate AI ROI — down from ~50% the prior year. Only ~6% of organizations qualify as high performers extracting bottom-line value. Implication: Mass adoption is running ahead of measurable output, and the gap may be widening.
Source: Growth Unhinged — https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2026-state-of-ai-gtm-report

**Platform & Efficiency Signals**
- ICONIQ's 2026 State of Go-to-Market (150+ B2B GTM executives, portfolio operating data): High-AI-adoption companies run 43% fewer GTM FTEs at identical revenue bands ($10M–$25M ARR: 20 FTEs vs 35 for low-adoption peers; $25M–$100M: 45 vs 65). AE quota attainment: 67% at AI-forward companies vs 59% elsewhere. Net New Recurring Revenue as component of AE comp jumped from 25% to 33% of companies year-over-year — largest single-year shift ICONIQ tracked.
Source: ICONIQ Growth — https://www.iconiq.com/growth/reports/state-of-go-to-market-2026

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- DGR's 2026 ABM Benchmark Survey: 52% of B2B marketers say ABM is meeting expectations; 23% say exceeding. 56% prioritize new account acquisition. 47% now integrate ABM with demand generation. 84% use AI and intent data for ABM personalization. Signal: ABM has moved past pilot status into core operating model for majority of respondents.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/blog/2026-account-based-marketing-abm-benchmark-survey-findings-abm-moves-beyond-pilot-stage-with-ai-powering-smarter-execution/52996

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Zero-click resolution on high-intent B2B informational queries averaged 42% in Q1 2026, up from 31% in December 2025, across ~500 brands and 12,000 queries. AI Overviews now appear on 82% of B2B tech queries. Implication: The window between buyer research and click-through is narrowing faster than most SEO forecasts projected six months ago.
Source: Omnibound AI — https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/ai-seo-statistics

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- MarTech consolidation activity up 13% over 2024 levels. Canva acquired Ortto (customer data + marketing automation) and Simtheory (AI collaboration/agents), signaling visual-first platforms are building end-to-end marketing stacks. Adobe announced general availability of CX Enterprise Coworker (agentic AI for marketing outcomes).
Source: Solutions Review — https://solutionsreview.com/crm/2026/06/12/top-martech-news-from-the-week-of-june-12th/ | House of MarTech — https://houseofmartech.com/blog/2026-martech-vendor-consolidation-wave-what-the-acquisitions-mean-for-your-stack-strategy

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Global managed security market projected at $93B (2025) to $106B (2026), 14.4% growth. Buying committees for cybersecurity decisions grew from 4–6 stakeholders in 2020 to 6–10 in 2026. Cyber-insurance underwriters now heavily scrutinizing RMM hardening and blast-radius controls — signaling buyers are demanding operational accountability and internal security posture evidence, not just vendor certifications.
Source: Huntress — https://www.huntress.com/msp-guide/msp-security-industry-trends
