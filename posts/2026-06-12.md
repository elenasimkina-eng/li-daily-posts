## LinkedIn Post

For the first time, B2B teams can see exactly how often their content appears inside Google's AI answers.

Google added AI performance reports to Search Console on June 3: impressions, pages, countries, devices. You can now separate AI Overviews visibility from traditional organic visibility in the same tool. (Search Engine Land — https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-ai-performance-reports-and-controls-to-block-your-content-in-ai-responses-479298)

No click data.

That absence matters more than it looks. AI Mode runs at a 93% zero-click rate. Impression counts tell you you're visible. They don't tell you traffic is coming, because structurally it mostly isn't.

Meanwhile, the channel where B2B buyers are actually forming vendor shortlists — direct LLM conversations in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude — produces zero data for any analytics platform. 89% of B2B buyers use those tools during research, and none of it shows up anywhere. (MarketBetter, 2026 Dark Funnel Analysis — https://marketbetter.ai/blog/b2b-dark-funnel-capture-invisible-buyers-2026/)

You can now see your AI impressions. You still can't see your AI shortlist.

## Central Insight
For the first time, B2B teams can see AI Overviews impression data directly in Search Console. But the reports show no click data — in a channel with a 93% zero-click rate. And the channel where vendor shortlists are actually formed (direct LLM queries in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) produces zero data for any analytics platform. The measurement tool that just arrived covers the less dangerous of the two AI blind spots.

## Why This Matters
- B2B demand gen teams now have their first AI-specific visibility data, but it measures impressions in a near-zero-click channel — not the pipeline input they need
- The distinction between "AI impressions" (visible) and "AI shortlist" (formed in LLM conversations, invisible) creates a structural false-confidence risk for teams interpreting high AI impression counts as strong AI presence
- The 89% of B2B buyers using LLMs during research generate zero signals for marketing analytics — the exact channel that decides early-stage vendor inclusion is also the most measurement-resistant
