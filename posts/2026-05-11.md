## LinkedIn Post

5WPR released the AI Platform Citation Source Index 2026, synthesizing 680 million individual citations across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude. A companion study documents what may be the sharpest structural break in B2B search in years: the overlap between top Google-ranking pages and AI-cited sources has dropped from 70% to under 20%.

That number reframes what "visibility" means for demand generation programs.

Several patterns stand out. The top 15 domains capture 68% of all AI citation share — a concentration that exceeds anything Google's PageRank produced at comparable scale. For B2B Technology specifically, AI Overviews now trigger on 70% of relevant queries. SEO Clarity's research on 68,000 queries shows the performance outcome is binary: pages cited inside AI Overviews see 35% more organic clicks; pages not cited see CTR declines of 15–46% for the same queries.

The decoupling runs deeper than platform preference. Only 11% of domains cited by ChatGPT are also cited by Perplexity. A single "AI-optimized" content strategy does not transfer cleanly across platforms.

There is also a timing asymmetry most demand gen teams have not priced in: new content can enter AI citation pools in 3–5 days, compared to 3–6 months for Google ranking. That changes the investment case for earned media placements significantly.

The structural question this raises: most B2B demand gen programs are still built around one visibility model — organic search rank — while the channel where high-intent B2B research is increasingly happening now operates on separate logic, separate sources, and separate timelines.

Running one program and measuring the other produces an attribution gap that compounds quietly.

## Central Insight
SEO rank and AI citation share have decoupled structurally. A brand that ranks #1 on Google cannot assume it will appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini answers. For B2B demand gen, this creates two separate visibility programs with different logic, different timelines, and different measurement requirements — and most teams are running only one.

- The top 15 domains capture 68% of all AI citation share — a winner-take-most dynamic that rewards source concentration, not keyword coverage

- For B2B Technology specifically, AI Overviews appear in 70% of queries, making citation status a binary performance outcome: cited pages get 35% more clicks; non-cited pages lose 15–46% of clicks

Source: PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-5w-research-overlap-between-top-google-rankings-and-ai-cited-sources-has-collapsed-from-70-to-under-20-302760132.html

Source: AuthorityTech — https://authoritytech.io/blog/chatgpt-vs-perplexity-vs-google-ai-overviews-b2b-pipeline-2026

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google AI Overviews now appear in 25.8% of all US searches. B2B Technology faces the highest AI Overview exposure at 70%. Non-cited sites see 15–46% CTR declines when AI Overviews appear. Sites cited within AI Overviews see 35% more organic clicks — creating a bifurcated performance outcome depending entirely on citation status.
Source: SEO Clarity — https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- Across 478 B2B companies, GTM effectiveness has fallen from 78% in 2018 to 47% in 2025. Approximately 83–84% of opportunities now end in "no decision." Across a basket of ~500 public companies, roughly 53% of GTM spend is estimated to be ineffective.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/the-future-of-gtm-starts-with-causal-clarity/

- Buying committees for deals over $50K now average 11.2 stakeholders (up from 9.7 in 2024), lengthening mid-market sales cycles to 121 days and enterprise cycles to 218 days. AI-assisted SDR/content workflows show a 38% reduction in cost-per-lead and 2.4x more meetings booked per rep.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/feature/demand-gen-reports-2026-b2b-trends-research-report-is-live/52002-2/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- The Google AI Overview CTR impact is asymmetric: non-cited pages lose 15–46% of clicks depending on query type; informational queries show 30–40% organic traffic declines for non-cited pages. B2B Technology is the most exposed vertical, with AI Overviews triggering on 70% of relevant queries.
Source: Stackmatix — https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/google-ai-overview-seo-impact

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- MarTech (May 2026) argues the structural decline in GTM effectiveness is measurement-driven: the majority of B2B teams still use touch-based attribution, which systematically over- or under-credits programs. Causal models using holdout-based incrementality are being positioned as the next standard — the claim being that 53% of spend inefficiency is invisible under existing models.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/the-gtm-spending-problem-no-one-wants-to-admit/

- LinkedIn now influences 28.3% of new B2B business deals, up from 15% the prior year. The average B2B customer journey has extended to 272 days (up from 211), with buyers entering the sales pipeline later but with higher intent. LinkedIn's ROAS across measured accounts is 121% vs. 67% for Google Search and 51% for Meta.
Source: Dreamdata / 42 Agency — https://intel.42agency.com/b2b-benchmarks/linkedin-ads-benchmarks/
