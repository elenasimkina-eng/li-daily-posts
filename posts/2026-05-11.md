## LinkedIn Post

51% of B2B software buyers now begin their research in an AI chatbot — up from 29% just twelve months ago. That number is notable. What changes the strategic picture is what comes next.

G2 surveyed 1,076 B2B buyers in March 2026. AI chatbots are now the #1 source influencing vendor shortlists at 54%, ahead of review sites at 43% and vendor websites at 36%. 69% of buyers chose a different vendor than they initially planned based on AI guidance. One in three purchased from a vendor they had never heard of before.

The most consequential data point: 95% of winning vendors were on the buyer's Day-One shortlist. The top-ranked vendor in that first list wins 77% of the time.

The tension here isn't about channel mix. It's about timing.

Most demand gen programs are architected around an observable research phase — traffic signals, content engagement, intent data activating, SDR sequences launching. The assumption is that buyers are reachable while they're still forming opinions.

But the shortlist is now being built inside AI conversations that leave no analytics trail. By the time a buyer's intent signal registers in your tools, the consideration set may already be closed.

This starts to look less like a new channel and more like a pre-funnel that runs parallel to everything demand gen can currently measure.

For B2B cybersecurity vendors serving MSPs — where buying committees have grown to 8+ stakeholders and peer credibility anchors every evaluation — the question isn't just "are we ranking?" It's whether we appear in the AI conversations that happen before any formal evaluation begins.

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- G2's "Answer Economy" report (1,076 B2B buyers surveyed March 2026; 39 qualitative interviews) found AI chatbots are now the #1 source influencing vendor shortlists at 54%, ahead of software review sites (43%) and vendor websites (36%). 51% of B2B buyers now begin research with AI chatbots — up from 29% in April 2025. Implication: AI-generated shortlists are forming before any demand gen touchpoint registers.
Source: G2 / PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-g2-research-half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots-302742807.html

- 69% of buyers chose a different vendor than initially planned based on AI chatbot guidance; 1 in 3 purchased from a vendor they'd never heard of before. The Day-One shortlist is decisive: 95% of winning vendors were on it, and the top-ranked vendor wins 77% of deals.
Source: G2 / PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-g2-research-half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots-302742807.html

- eMarketer noted this creates a pre-intent visibility problem: buyers form vendor opinions before any measurable intent signal fires, making traditional demand capture tools structurally late to the evaluation.
Source: eMarketer — https://www.emarketer.com/content/chatbots-guide-b2b-software-research-raising-stakes-brand-visibility

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google moved AI Max for Search to general availability April 15, 2026. Dynamic Search Ads will auto-upgrade to AI Max beginning September 2026 (mandatory). Google reports campaigns using the full AI Max feature suite average 7% more conversions at similar CPA/ROAS. Key change for B2B: final URL expansion is now default — Google can route traffic to pages other than those specified. Early B2B results show high variance; one advertiser reported a 0.76% conversion rate, their worst-performing match type.
Source: Google Ads Blog — https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/dsa-upgrade-to-ai-max-2026/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- The G2 data reconfigures ABM timing assumptions. If 95% of winning vendors are on the Day-One shortlist and that list is now formed in AI conversations before intent signals appear, ABM programs that activate on intent data may be entering accounts where the consideration set is already closed. MarTech framed this as "the new B2B battleground."
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/the-new-b2b-battleground-is-getting-on-ais-shortlist/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- B2B Technology sector now has 82% AI Overview presence in Google results, up from 36% one year ago. The overlap between top-10 Google rankings and AI Overview citations has collapsed from 75% in mid-2025 to 17–38% by early 2026 — high organic rankings no longer predict AI answer-layer visibility in B2B tech categories.
Source: Search Land Insider — https://www.searchlandinsider.com/google-handed-your-organic-traffic-to-its-ai-heres-what-the-data-shows/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- GTM effectiveness across 478 B2B companies has fallen from 78% in 2018 to 47% in 2025 as martech stacks have grown without proportional output. The 2026 martech landscape grew only 0.7% (15,384 to 15,505 tools), signaling consolidation. 90.3% of marketing organizations now use AI agents but only 23.3% have deployed them fully.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/martech-2026-ai-drives-a-major-industry-reset/

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Managed security services is the fastest-growing MSP segment at 18% annual growth (vs. 14% overall MSP market). 87% of MSPs plan to increase AI spending in 2026. Cybersecurity buying committees have expanded to 8+ stakeholders. Trust, operational resilience, and AI maturity proof are now top three evaluation criteria — with peer networks and analyst citations as the most influential research channels, which feed into AI chatbot training data.
Source: MSSP Alert — https://www.msspalert.com/perspective/why-2026-is-a-turning-point-for-msp-cybersecurity
