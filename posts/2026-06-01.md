## LinkedIn Post

The channels closing deals fastest in B2B right now leave almost no tracking signal.

Deals entering through invisible touchpoints — AI research conversations, dark social, communities — close in about 61 days versus 131 days for tracked deals. Attribution models have no record of how those deals started. (MarketBetter — https://marketbetter.ai/blog/b2b-dark-funnel-capture-invisible-buyers-2026/)

AI search is compressing the discovery layer further upstream. Walker Sands analyzed 828 enterprise B2B companies across 45 million keywords and found that AI Overviews appear in nearly 50% of relevant search result pages, yet the average enterprise brand is cited in just 3% of those AI-generated answers. (Walker Sands B2B AI Search Visibility Benchmark — https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/01/3266771/0/en/Walker-Sands-Launches-B2B-AI-Search-Visibility-Benchmark-to-Measure-Enterprise-Brand-Presence-in-AI-Generated-Search-Results.html) Buyers are forming impressions before they land anywhere trackable.

Today, Google Ads cuts granular reporting history to 37 months, removing the long-term performance baselines teams have used for seasonal benchmarking and multi-year comparisons. (Google Ads Developer Blog — https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2026/05/new-data-retention-policy-for-google.html) On its own, a minor policy change. Arriving alongside everything else, a signal worth noting.

The pattern: the highest-quality buyer signals live in channels with the least tracking infrastructure, while teams keep investing in measuring the shrinking slice of pipeline they can actually see.

"We optimize what we can measure" used to be a best practice. It's starting to look like a structural constraint.

## Central Insight
B2B demand gen teams are investing more in measurement infrastructure at the precise moment the signal environment is getting structurally harder to read: the highest-converting channels (AI research, dark social, communities) leave the least trackable data, while historical paid media baselines are now formally capped.

## Why This Matters
- Programs built on what can be measured may be systematically underweighting what actually drives pipeline
- AI-driven buyer research creates a category of warm, high-intent accounts that arrive with no observable journey — and they close faster
- The Google data retention change is not merely a data hygiene issue; it removes the long-cycle measurement infrastructure B2B teams depend on for seasonal and annual benchmarking

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- Walker Sands analyzed 828 enterprise B2B companies across nearly 45 million relevant search keywords in 14 technology industries. Key finding: AI Overviews appear in nearly 50% of relevant search result pages, yet the average enterprise B2B brand is cited in just 3% of those AI-generated answers. Cybersecurity leads all 14 industries in brand citation rates within AI-generated results; professional services ranks lowest. Implication: AI visibility is both industry-dependent and severely underpenetrated — even for the best-performing sector.
Source: GlobeNewswire (Walker Sands) — https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/01/3266771/0/en/Walker-Sands-Launches-B2B-AI-Search-Visibility-Benchmark-to-Measure-Enterprise-Brand-Presence-in-AI-Generated-Search-Results.html

- 2X AI Visibility Index (April 2026), based on analysis of 70 B2B companies: 96% of B2B companies are invisible in AI-driven buyer discovery. Only 4.3% maintain a healthy discovery funnel where their brand appears in early-stage buyer questions. The remainder surface only when buyers already know the company name.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/2x-survey-finds-96-of-b2b-companies-are-invisible-in-ai-discovery/52536/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google Ads data retention policy takes effect June 1, 2026 (today): granular hourly, daily, and weekly reporting data is now capped at 37 months. Monthly, quarterly, and annual data remains available for 11 years. Multi-year comparative analysis at the day or week level — seasonal baselines, long-cycle B2B performance patterns, year-over-year campaign comparisons — is no longer accessible through standard API queries once data ages past the 37-month window.
Source: Google Ads Developer Blog — https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2026/05/new-data-retention-policy-for-google.html

- LinkedIn 2026 algorithm: company page reach down 60–66% since 2024. Personal profiles generate 8x more engagement. Thought Leader Ads deliver $2.29 median CPC vs. $10.24 for standard sponsored content; 3x higher CTR; 40–50% lower CPL. AI-generated generic content is being deprioritized; original perspective is rewarded.
Source: ALM Corp — https://almcorp.com/blog/linkedin-feed-algorithm-update-llm-2026/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- 2026 ABM Benchmark Survey (Demand Gen Report): 47% of respondents identify personalized content as the ABM tactic delivering highest ROI, above all others. AI effectiveness score: 7.3/10 for improving ABM outcomes. Nearly 80% of organizations actively executing ABM; 52% meeting expectations, 23% exceeding.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/resources/read-the-2026-account-based-marketing-benchmark-survey-results/53096/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Post-Google I/O 2026: AI Mode powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash now default globally. Only 17–54% of AI Overview citations come from top-10 organic results — ranking first no longer guarantees inclusion in AI-generated answers. Brands appearing inside AI Overviews see 91% more paid clicks than brands absent from them.
Source: Fox Agency — https://fox.agency/us/insights/google-i-o-2026-what-it-means-for-b2b-seo/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- Dark funnel acceleration: 73% of the B2B buyer's journey happens in channels invisible to standard analytics (AI conversations, communities, dark social). Deals entering through invisible touchpoints close in approximately 60.6 days versus 131 days for deals entering through tracked channels — 54% faster. Standard form-based attribution models capture less than 30% of the actual buyer journey.
Source: MarketBetter — https://marketbetter.ai/blog/b2b-dark-funnel-capture-invisible-buyers-2026/

- Intent data quality gap: 91% of B2B marketers now use intent data to prioritize accounts; only 24% report exceptional ROI. First-party signals carry near-real-time precision; third-party data runs 60–90 day latency and relies on modeled cooperative signals. Adoption has dramatically outrun execution discipline.
Source: FL0 — https://fl0.com/blog/first-party-vs-third-party-b2b-intent-data-benchmark-study-2026
