## LinkedIn Post

The highest-converting discovery channel in B2B right now isn't Google. It's AI search.

AI-generated answers now account for 17% of B2B SaaS discovery, up from 4% a year ago. Traffic from those citations converts at 14.2%, vs. 2.8% for Google organic — a five-to-one advantage that's still widening. (Data-Mania, 2026 AI Search Visibility Benchmarks — https://www.data-mania.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-benchmarks-2026-citation-rates-share-of-voice-b2b-saas/)

Meanwhile, 81% of B2B marketing leaders say AI visibility is a blind spot in their organization. Only 10% can consistently connect AI-driven touchpoints to revenue. (Agentcy Annual AI Visibility Index, n=104, February 2026 — https://ppc.land/most-b2b-marketers-cant-prove-ais-role-in-their-pipeline/)

67% of B2B teams still run last-touch attribution — a model built for a buyer journey that assumed a few trackable clicks. The actual journey now spans 272 days and 88 touchpoints. Teams that have upgraded — running MMM, multi-touch, or hybrid — report 1.6× larger marketing-sourced pipeline. (Improvado, 2026 B2B Attribution Report — https://improvado.io/blog/b2b-marketing-attribution)

This is a budget allocation problem wearing a measurement problem as cover.

The demand gen playbook is optimizing away from its fastest-growing, highest-converting traffic source. The attribution model says it doesn't exist.

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- 90.3% of marketing organizations now use AI agents somewhere in their martech stack; content production agents lead at 68.9% adoption. Teams adopting agent workflows report 27% faster campaign build times and 19% lower cost per qualified lead — but the attribution infrastructure to capture their pipeline contribution largely doesn't exist.
Source: OneReach.ai — https://onereach.ai/blog/agentic-ai-adoption-rates-roi-market-trends/

- 17% of all B2B SaaS discovery now happens through AI-generated answers, up from just 4% a year ago. AI search traffic converts at 14.2% vs. Google organic's 2.8% — an AI citation is worth roughly 5x a traditional organic click in conversion terms.
Source: Data-Mania — https://www.data-mania.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-benchmarks-2026-citation-rates-share-of-voice-b2b-saas/

- Only 10% of B2B marketing leaders can consistently connect AI-driven touchpoints to revenue; 81% say AI visibility is a blind spot in their organization. (Agentcy Annual AI Visibility Index, February 2026, n=104 senior B2B marketing leaders)
Source: PPC.land — https://ppc.land/most-b2b-marketers-cant-prove-ais-role-in-their-pipeline/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm now weights depth signals: reading time, comment back-and-forth, saves, and direct shares. A post generating 20 substantive comments now outperforms one with 200 reactions in algorithmic distribution. External links penalized by 60%.
Source: Cleverly — https://www.cleverly.co/blog/linkedin-algorithm

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- Median MQL-to-SQL conversion rate across B2B programs is 13%, meaning 87% of MQLs don't advance to sales qualification. Median pipeline coverage settled at 3.2x quota; top quartile runs at 4.8x. The gap between these tiers is widening.
Source: Omnibound — https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/b2b-demand-generation-statistics

- ABM users report 2x faster pipeline growth (35% annually) and 20%+ increase in pipeline velocity. 84% of companies see measurable pipeline growth from ABM programs.
Source: Influ2 — https://www.influ2.com/blog/account-based-marketing-stats

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- AI Overviews appear on 48% of SERPs. Brands cited within AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than non-cited brands — the citation advantage compounds across both organic and paid channels simultaneously.
Source: Click-Vision — https://click-vision.com/zero-click-search-statistics

- ChatGPT's share of AI search referrals has declined while Perplexity and Google AI Mode are growing. The platform mix for AI citations is fragmenting, making multi-engine GEO strategies necessary for consistent visibility.
Source: Goodie AI — https://higoodie.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-report-2026/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- 38% of B2B pipeline (median) comes from dark-funnel sources like podcasts, communities, and dark social that leave no digital tracking signal — rising to 51% for PLG-led motions. Yet 67% of B2B marketing teams still rely on last-touch attribution, despite the average buyer journey spanning 272 days and 88 touchpoints.
Source: Improvado — https://improvado.io/blog/b2b-marketing-attribution

- Teams running at least one of multi-touch attribution, MMM, or hybrid with holdout fidelity report 1.6× larger marketing-sourced pipeline than teams relying on last-touch only.
Source: Improvado — https://improvado.io/blog/b2b-marketing-attribution

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Cybersecurity is now the fastest-growing MSP service segment at 18% annual growth through 2026 vs. 14% for overall MSP market. 92.5% of MSPs see AI-driven threat intelligence as a key growth driver; over 80% now offer AI-enhanced security services (XDR, SIEM).
Source: Accio — https://www.accio.com/business/msp-market-trends

- The typical cybersecurity buying committee grew from 4-6 stakeholders (2020) to 6-10 by 2026. When an MSP recommends a vendor, the sales cycle is 50-70% shorter and close rate is 2-3x higher than direct sales.
Source: Sagiss — https://www.sagiss.com/blog/msp-industry-statistics-trends/
