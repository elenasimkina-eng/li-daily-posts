## LinkedIn Post

Half of B2B software buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot, not Google. That's from G2's 2026 Answer Economy Report — 1,076 decision makers, global sample.

The more unsettling number: 69% of those buyers ended up choosing a vendor different from the one they started with. One in three bought from a company they'd never heard of.

The vendor shortlist is forming before your SDRs email anyone. Before your retargeting fires. Before a prospect lands on a page you paid to rank.

And most teams aren't visible where that shortlist is being built. DerivateX ran 1,400 buyer-intent prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini — 44% of B2B SaaS companies scored below 50 out of 100 on AI presence. The gap between the highest-scoring company (89) and the lowest (2) was 87 points.

At the same time, DemandScience's 2026 Performance Marketing Report found that 87% of organizations say their marketing investments produce signals that don't reflect real buying intent — and only 26% of those signals convert to qualified opportunities.

What that combination suggests: teams are generating activity metrics in a system that a growing portion of buyers are bypassing. The dashboard looks fine. The shortlist forms somewhere else.

This isn't a content quality problem or a budget problem. It's a discovery layer problem. And it's structural.

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- G2's "Answer Economy" report (1,076 B2B decision makers) found 51% of B2B software buyers now begin vendor research with an AI chatbot, overtaking Google as the primary starting point for the first time. 69% of those buyers ended up choosing a vendor different from the one they started with. 33% purchased from a vendor they weren't previously familiar with. 85% of buyers report thinking more highly of a vendor when an AI chatbot recommends them — but 64% also encounter inaccurate AI recommendations often or very often.
Source: G2 / PRNewswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-g2-research-half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots-302742807.html

- DerivateX benchmark study ran 1,400 buyer-intent prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini against 50 B2B SaaS companies. Average AI Presence Score: 56.9/100. 44% of companies scored below 50. The gap between highest scorer (89) and lowest (2) is 87 points — despite both operating in active software categories with running marketing teams. Visibility is driven primarily by mention rate and position prominence (60 of 100 available scoring points).
Source: DemandGenReport — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/derivatex-study-finds-b2b-saas-companies-are-invisible-to-ai-assisted-buyers/52463/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads (TLAs) now average 2.68% CTR versus 0.42% for standard single-image ads — a 6x efficiency differential. CPC: $2.29 for TLAs vs $13.23 for single image (77% cheaper per click). Analysis based on 17.4M impressions, 6,280 ads, $3.5M in spend over 15 months across a B2B SaaS portfolio. Budget allocation sweet spot: 25–50% TLA allocation delivers 5.08% CTR at $0.84 CPC. TLAs now account for a growing share of Sponsored Content impressions alongside Document Ads.
Source: Fractional Demand — https://fractionaldemand.com/resources/blog/thought-leader-ads-2026

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- DemandScience's 2026 State of Performance Marketing Report (750 senior marketing leaders, $100M–$5B+ orgs) found: 87% of organizations say their marketing investments produce unreliable or inflated intent signals (clicks, downloads, behavioral scores). Only 26% of those intent signals convert to qualified opportunities. Two-thirds of leaders say their dashboards show success that fails to translate into revenue. 25% of marketing budget is estimated as wasted. 76% produce content not informed by verified buyer signals. The report calls this structural gap the "Marketing Data Mirage."
Source: DemandScience / GlobeNewswire — https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/17/3206933/0/en/The-2026-State-of-Performance-Marketing-Report-How-Inflated-Signals-AI-Noise-and-Disconnected-Tools-Are-Derailing-B2B-Growth.html

- DemandGenReport's 2026 ABM Benchmark Survey: AI personalization tool adoption inside ABM programs jumped from 11% (Q1 2024) to 38% (Q1 2026). ABM lifts tier-1 account engagement 3.4x over non-ABM cohorts. AI effectiveness score averages 7.3/10 for improving ABM campaign outcomes.
Source: DemandGenReport — https://www.demandgenreport.com/blog/2026-account-based-marketing-abm-benchmark-survey-findings-abm-moves-beyond-pilot-stage-with-ai-powering-smarter-execution/52996

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- B2B technology sector faces the highest AI Overview exposure of any vertical — approximately 70% of typical B2B query types now trigger AI Overviews. The overlap between top-10 Google rankings and AI Overview citations has collapsed from 75% (mid-2025) to 17–38% by early 2026, meaning most top-ranked pages are not the ones cited in AI answers.
Source: Stackmatix — https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/google-ai-overview-seo-impact

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- Analysis across 478 B2B companies (MarTech): GTM effectiveness has fallen from 78% (2018) to 47% (2025). 83–84% of opportunities now end in "no decision." Less than $0.50 of each GTM dollar is producing effective outcomes. Authors note the decline is structural, not cyclical — driven by shifts in buyer behavior, not deficiencies in the tools themselves.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/the-future-of-gtm-starts-with-causal-clarity/

- 72% of marketing leaders surveyed say AI-generated content is hurting brand distinctiveness — a constraint signal emerging as AI content volume scales across the B2B ecosystem.
Source: DemandScience / GlobeNewswire — https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/17/3206933/0/en/The-2026-State-of-Performance-Marketing-Report-How-Inflated-Signals-AI-Noise-and-Disconnected-Tools-Are-Derailing-B2B-Growth.html

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- 77% of MSPs cite lack of client urgency as their primary sales challenge — technical teams can identify prospect security weaknesses but struggle to translate risk into business-language arguments that drive investment. Cybersecurity is the fastest-growing MSP service segment at 18% annual growth. Managed security services market projected: $38.31B (2025) → $69.16B (2030).
Source: The Hacker News — https://thehackernews.com/2026/05/top-five-sales-challenges-costing-msps.html
