## LinkedIn Post

93% of Google AI Mode sessions end without users clicking to an external site.

That figure comes from Semrush data, surfaced last week when Adobe launched Brand Visibility, a new GEO platform for enterprise teams (MarTech: https://martech.org/adobe-brings-geo-to-cx-enterprise-with-adobe-brand-visibility/).

B2B funnels assume visibility produces traffic. Traffic feeds intent signals, retargeting logic, nurture sequences. If buyers are forming opinions inside AI engines without ever leaving, the funnel doesn't stall. It never starts.

Being findable and being cited are no longer the same thing.

## Central Insight
When 93% of AI-powered search sessions end without a click to an external site, the foundational assumption of B2B demand gen — that visibility produces traffic, which produces pipeline — stops being reliable.

## Why This Matters
- Most B2B demand gen programs are built around driving qualified traffic to owned properties; if buyers form opinions inside AI engines without clicking out, the measurement and activation layer never fires
- This isn't a traffic optimization problem — it's a structural shift in where buying decisions form, before any marketing system can detect them
- Being cited in AI engines and being findable via traditional SEO are now two separate disciplines with different levers

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- Adobe launched Adobe Brand Visibility on June 17, 2026 — an enterprise GEO platform drawing on 289 million real-world AI search prompts (via Semrush acquisition). Semrush data embedded in the launch reveals 93% of Google AI Mode sessions end without users clicking to an external site. For B2B demand gen teams, this means the dominant AI-powered search surface is producing no traffic signal to act on.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/adobe-brings-geo-to-cx-enterprise-with-adobe-brand-visibility/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google completed its May 2026 core update on June 2. The update advances the Helpful Content system with AI-driven evaluation of depth, tone, and user satisfaction. B2B pages built around keyword coverage rather than substantive answers face continued ranking pressure through Q3.
Source: Connectica — https://www.connecticallc.com/google-algorithm-updates/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- LeanData's State of GTM Efficiency Report finds 89% of B2B organizations generate AI signals but have not connected them to reliable lead routing and assignment. Only 11% have built AI-powered routing in production. Signal collection has been solved; activation has not.
Source: LeanData — https://www.leandata.com/resources/state-of-gtm-efficiency-report/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- 10Fold June 2026 research: B2B tech sectors are adapting to AI search at materially different speeds based on buyer risk profiles and proof requirements. Regulated and cybersecurity verticals are adapting slowest. "AI search has created a common mandate, but not a common playbook."
Source: Business Wire — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260610133126/en/10Fold-Research-Shows-There-is-a-Vertical-Divide-in-B2B-Content-Readiness

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- MarTech.org analysis: 90.3% of marketing organizations now use AI agents; only 23.3% have deployed in full production. AI capability has advanced materially (AI systems can now plan and execute full campaign workflows autonomously) but marketing orgs haven't restructured to operationalize it. The bottleneck shifted from AI capability to organizational readiness.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/ai-moved-forward-marketing-did-not/

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Global managed security market projected to grow from $93B to $106B in 2026 (14.4%). Cybersecurity product sales through MSPs expected to grow 15%. Mature SMB buyers now evaluate MSPs on process evidence — incident response process, after-hours monitoring, admin access controls — rather than tool stack breadth.
Source: NMS Consulting — https://nmsconsulting.com/it-managed-services-market-size-2026/
