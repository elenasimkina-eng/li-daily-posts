## LinkedIn Post

The first cybersecurity-specific AI search visibility benchmark just published, and the headline number is harder to shake than expected.

73% of cybersecurity vendors tested received zero citations from ChatGPT when buyers searched for vendor recommendations in their category. Not low visibility. Zero.

Below that sits something more disorienting: citation rates vary 46x across platforms. ChatGPT cites brands 0.59% of the time; Perplexity sits at 13.05%. Teams tracking one have no idea where they stand in the others. (GrackerAI, 100 vendors, 250 buyer prompts — https://gracker.ai/data-and-research-reports/state-of-ai-search-visibility-cybersecurity-2026)

The asset type that built cybersecurity pipelines for the last decade — gated whitepapers, form-gated benchmarks, downloadable ebooks — is exactly what AI engines cannot crawl. Content updated within 30 days gets 3.2x more citations. Most cybersecurity content is neither ungated nor current.

It starts looking less like a visibility problem and more like a content architecture mismatch. Buyers moved to AI-mediated research. The content that could earn citations sits behind forms no LLM can access.

The benchmark that matters now is not "rank #1 for this keyword." It is "appear in the answer when a CISO asks ChatGPT what to evaluate."
