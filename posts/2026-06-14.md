## LinkedIn Post

Ranking first in Google search now costs you clicks.

AI Overviews appear in 82% of B2B technology searches, up from 36% in 2025, per SeoProfy's 2026 AI Overviews analysis. When one is present, the top organic result loses 34.5% of its expected CTR. Brands cited inside those overviews gain 35% in brand clicks.

So the #1 position now carries a structural penalty unless you're also appearing in the summary above the results.

For B2B demand generation, this matters because commercial-intent searches (the queries that feed pipeline) are heavily represented in that 82%. Cybersecurity and B2B tech have the highest AI Overview exposure of any vertical.

The practical shift: ranking authority and citation authority are now different problems. Teams treating them as the same thing are optimizing for a metric that no longer fully predicts traffic.
