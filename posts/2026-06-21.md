## LinkedIn Post

The assumption that organized most B2B martech stacks is quietly inverting.

Standard architecture for the last decade: applications on top, data underneath. CDPs, MAPs, and intent tools running above a shared warehouse. Data was infrastructure. Apps were where strategy lived.

Databricks challenged that directly. CustomerLake, launched June 16 at the Data + AI Summit, embeds the CDP into the data lakehouse as a native layer, not as an integration sitting above the data. From their launch post (databricks.com/blog/introducing-customerlake-agentic-cdp): "A CDP sitting outside the data platform will always be too slow and too incomplete for agents running in milliseconds."

Salesforce's $3.6B acquisition of Fin, announced June 15, follows the same logic from the CRM layer. Fin's AI Agent resolves 76% of support volume end-to-end. Rather than maintaining it as a separate integration, Salesforce is pulling that capability into the core system.

Scott Brinker named the broader pattern in his April ChiefMartec newsletter: martech value is migrating from the application layer to infrastructure — data, identity, orchestration, governance.

The customer data layer is being rebuilt from the warehouse up, not from marketing applications down.

For demand gen teams, the relevant question is shifting from "which tools are you running?" to "how well is your data model actually built?" Most stacks were assembled for the first question, while the second quietly became the constraint.

## Central Insight
Three structural moves in one week — Databricks embedding a CDP into its data lakehouse (June 16), Salesforce acquiring Fin for $3.6B to absorb AI customer service into CRM (June 15), and Scott Brinker's thesis that martech value is migrating from apps to infrastructure — describe the same directional shift: the customer data layer is being rebuilt from the warehouse up, not from marketing applications down. For demand gen teams, the competitive question is shifting from "which tools" to "how well is your data model built."

## Why This Matters
- Most B2B demand gen teams assembled best-of-breed tool stacks on the assumption that applications layer on top of data — that assumption is being structurally challenged by data infra companies entering the application space
- The agentic marketing model (continuous campaigns, real-time personalization at scale) requires data and execution to live in the same layer — traditional martech architectures create latency that breaks this
- Teams that treat this as a procurement question ("which CDP should we buy") rather than an architecture question will optimize for the wrong variable
