## LinkedIn Post

DerivateX published benchmark data testing 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini — 1,400 buyer-intent prompts in total (https://derivatex.agency/report/ai-visibility-b2b-saas-2026). The average AI Presence Score came in at 56.9 out of 100. Forty-four percent of companies scored below 50. The gap between the highest and lowest scorer was 87 points — despite both operating in established categories with active marketing programs.

A separate multi-source analysis found that 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools in their purchase research, and that AI-sourced traffic converts at 14.2%, compared to 2.8% for Google organic. A 5.1x difference.

What stands out is the disconnect between where demand generation investment is concentrated and where the citation signal actually comes from. The strongest predictor of appearing in AI answers is not SEO rank or domain authority — it is how frequently a brand is mentioned across authoritative sources, regardless of whether those mentions include a backlink. Search Engine Land's analysis reinforces this: 47% of AI Overview citations come from pages ranking below position 5 organically (https://searchengineland.com/ai-visibility-starts-before-search-ends-with-citations-476308).

The pattern looks less like a channel shift and more like a measurement gap. Seventy-eight percent of marketing teams do not yet track AI visibility or AI-sourced traffic. Which means the majority of demand generation programs have no signal on a channel that already influences most B2B buyer research — and converts at over five times the rate of organic search.

For high-consideration B2B categories like cybersecurity, the first set of vendor names appearing in those early AI conversations may anchor the entire evaluation process before any sales contact occurs.

## Central Insight
AI-assisted buyer research converts at 5.1x the rate of Google organic search, yet 44% of B2B SaaS companies score below 50 on AI visibility — because AI citation is driven by brand mention frequency across authoritative sources, not SEO rank, which is where most demand generation programs are still optimized and measured.

## Why This Matters
- Most B2B demand generation programs are built around improving search rank, but 73% of buyers now use AI tools in research and those tools don't privilege highly-ranked pages — 47% of AI Overview citations come from pages outside the top 5
- AI-sourced traffic converts at 14.2% vs. 2.8% for Google organic, making AI citation a pipeline quality issue, not just a brand awareness issue
- Only 22% of marketing teams track AI visibility or AI-sourced traffic, meaning the channel with the highest conversion rate in B2B research has essentially no measurement coverage in most organizations
