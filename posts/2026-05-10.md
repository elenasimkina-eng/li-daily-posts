## LinkedIn Post

Forrester's 2026 State of Business Buying report, based on surveys of nearly 18,000 global business buyers, surfaces a problem that sits at the center of most B2B demand generation programs.

94% of business buyers now use generative AI to inform their purchasing decisions. For purchases that involve genAI features — which increasingly includes cybersecurity and managed services products — the buying committee doubles in size: 14 members on average versus 7 for non-genAI purchases.

The standard demand gen response to larger committees is persona expansion and multi-touch nurture. But Forrester's GTM Singularity research, released at B2B Summit North America in April 2026, specifically names the tactics most companies are still running — mass email, MQL-qualified leads, gated content, siloed teams — as the practices "collapsing" under the combined pressure of AI and committee expansion.

The gated content piece carries a second-order implication. AI tools buyers now use for vendor research preferentially surface ungated content. A program built around gated assets is producing content that is structurally harder to surface through the research method 94% of buyers are already using.

The trial data adds another dimension: over 60% of buyers now complete a formal trial before committing. That means a significant share of buyers arrive at first vendor contact already deep into self-directed evaluation — which reframes where demand gen actually needs to operate.

Procurement professionals are decision-makers in 53% of buying cycles from the first interaction — a stakeholder category that rarely appears in standard demand gen persona matrices.

The pattern Forrester is describing isn't a future state. Buyers changed how they research and evaluate before most demand gen programs adapted to it.

## Central Insight
94% of B2B buyers use genAI in their research, but buying groups for genAI product purchases have doubled in size to 14 members — and the demand gen programs most companies still run (gated content, MQL capture, mass nurture) produce content that AI tools deprioritize in buyer research and targeting logic built for committees half this size.

## Why This Matters
- The asymmetry between buyer sophistication (94% using genAI) and seller GTM infrastructure (only 19% with genAI in production) means most demand gen programs are running legacy motions against an already-transformed buyer
- Buying committee doubling for genAI purchases is directly relevant to B2B cybersecurity vendors selling AI-enhanced products — the category where committee expansion is steepest
- Gated content is simultaneously a Forrester-identified failure tactic and structurally less discoverable to AI research tools, creating a compound penalty for programs that haven't ungated their top content
