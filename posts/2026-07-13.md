## LinkedIn Post

The assumption that ChatGPT is the new B2B discovery engine is directionally correct and tactically misleading.

Previsible's 2026 State of AI Discovery report analyzed 6.77 million LLM-driven sessions across 166 websites. ChatGPT commands 92.4% of all trackable standalone AI referral traffic — a dominant share. But the same dataset carries a quieter finding: Google's AI surfaces generate more AI-influenced brand discovery than every standalone LLM assistant combined.

Teams rebuilding their content strategy around "ChatGPT citations" may be optimizing for the most visible surface, not the largest one.

A second signal changes the ROI math. Brands cited inside Google AI Overviews receive 91% more paid clicks than non-cited competitors on the same SERP, not just more organic visibility (QuickSEO, 2026 AI Overviews data). For B2B technology queries — where AIOs appear 82% of the time — AIO citation is functioning as a trust signal that amplifies downstream ad performance.

AI visibility investment is starting to look like a paid media efficiency lever, not just an awareness play.

Investment optimized for Google AI surfaces compounds across organic and paid. Investment optimized for standalone LLMs captures measurable referral traffic from a smaller pool. Neither strategy is wrong. But teams treating them as equivalent bets are making asymmetric trade-offs on different evidence.

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- Previsible's 2026 State of AI Discovery Report (published July 6, 2026), analyzing 6.77 million LLM-driven sessions across 166 websites from November 2024 to May 2026, found ChatGPT commands 92.4% of all standalone AI referral traffic. The more significant finding: Google's AI surfaces (AI Overviews + AI Mode) generate more AI-influenced brand discovery than all standalone LLM assistants combined. Claude grew 64x during the tracked period and overtook Perplexity in March 2026; Copilot collapsed 96% from its peak. Implication: "optimize for ChatGPT" is correct for standalone LLMs but misses the larger AI discovery surface.
Source: BusinessWire — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260706755932/en/Previsibles-2026-State-of-AI-Discovery-Report-Finds-Google-Remains-the-Center-of-AI-Discovery-With-ChatGPT-Leading-Standalone-LLMs

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads now average 4.65% CTR versus 0.68% for all other LinkedIn formats — a 6.9x differential — at $0.51 CPC versus $2.42 for standard ads. The 25–50% budget-in-TL-Ads allocation tier produces 5.08% CTR at $0.84 CPC. LinkedIn's algorithm is structurally rewarding individual-voice content at an accelerating rate.
Source: Fractional Demand — https://fractionaldemand.com/resources/blog/thought-leader-ads-2026

- Google is testing AI-generated summaries displayed beneath sponsored ad descriptions in Search results, with a disclaimer noting summaries are generated independently of the advertiser. If broadly rolled out, this inserts an AI interpretation layer between ad copy and buyer click intent.
Source: Adapt Worldwide — https://www.adaptworldwide.com/2026/07/10/ai-search-news-roundup-june-july-2026/

**ABM & Demand Generation**

Integrating CRM, MAP, predictive scoring, and advertising tools produces a 53% conversion rate increase. Mature ABM programs convert at 22.33% MQA rate vs 14.19% for less mature — a 57% performance gap driven by infrastructure depth.
Source: Demandbase Labs — https://www.demandbase.com/resources/report/b2b-ai-gtm-benchmarks-report/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Google AI Overviews appear in 82% of B2B technology searches. Brands cited inside AIOs get 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than non-cited brands on the same SERP. The citation-to-paid-amplification dynamic means GEO investment is now generating paid media lift as a second-order effect — a dynamic not present in pre-AI search.
Source: QuickSEO — https://quickseo.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-statistics-2026-60-data-points-every-seo-should-know

Multi-touch attribution adoption grew from 31% (2023) to 47% (2026) but isn't solving the problem; teams running dual models — MTA for tactical + MMM for strategic — report the most defensible pipeline numbers.
