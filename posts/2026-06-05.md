## LinkedIn Post

For the first time, ranking #1 on Google and being visible inside Google's own answers have become two different things.

5W Research analyzed 680 million AI platform citations and found that the overlap between pages ranking in Google's top 10 and pages actually cited inside AI Overviews has collapsed — from 70% in mid-2025 to under 20% today.

That's not a gradual drift.

When Google AI Mode processes a query, it fans out into multiple sub-queries and retrieves the best-matching content fragment for each one. Citation is driven by topical specificity. The pages that rank aren't always the pages that answer.

The consequence for demand gen is a dashboard problem. Google Search Console still shows positions and impressions. The numbers look normal. But AI Overviews now reach 2.5 billion monthly users, and citation share in that layer is concentrated among 15 domains that together hold 68% of all citations across AI platforms.

Ranking and AI citation share are now two separate leaderboards. Most B2B programs are only tracking one.

## Central Insight
For the first time in the history of search, ranking in Google's top 10 and appearing in Google's AI-generated answers have become two separate, largely uncorrelated outcomes — and most B2B demand gen programs are only tracking one of them.

## Why This Matters
- B2B content and SEO investments are still evaluated primarily through keyword rankings and Google Search Console impressions — metrics that no longer predict AI citation visibility, where the actual early-stage buyer discovery now happens
- The citation concentration effect (top 15 domains capturing 68% of all AI citations) means the distribution of B2B visibility has compressed dramatically, and competitive position in AI Overviews cannot be inferred from organic ranking position
- With AI Overviews reaching 2.5 billion monthly users, the unchecked assumption that "if we rank, we're visible" is now materially wrong — creating a growing gap between reported SEO performance and actual buyer reach

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- Google AI Mode has surpassed 1 billion monthly users; 93% of queries processed through AI Mode end without a click — a rate far higher than the 43% zero-click rate for standard AI Overviews. The new behavior is structural, not a temporary usage spike.
