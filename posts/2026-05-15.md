## LinkedIn Post

Most LinkedIn campaigns get cut before they start working.

New benchmark data from ZenABM, across 211 B2B companies, shows LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads open at ~3.9% CTR in week one. By weeks 10–12, that number climbs above 8%. The performance cliff doesn't arrive until week 19. Most campaigns don't make it to week 6.

The unit economics already look unusual: median CPC of $2.29, versus $13.23 for single image ads and $15.61 for video. That's 6x cheaper per click before accounting for the compounding curve.

What's harder to explain to a creative director: candid, low-production content (a phone selfie, a whiteboard photo) outperforms studio-shot brand imagery by 45% in engagement on the same format. The production value that makes brand teams comfortable is actively working against performance.

Taken together, this starts looking less like a media buy and more like a content publishing strategy—one with a different optimization horizon than most paid social frameworks are designed to evaluate.

The assumption that paid LinkedIn performance is readable inside a 30-day sprint is starting to break.

Source: ZenABM TLA Benchmarks 2026, 211 companies — https://zenabm.com/blog/tla-benchmarks

## Central Insight
Thought Leader Ads on LinkedIn behave like a content publishing strategy, not a traditional paid media buy: CTR compounds from 3.9% to 8%+ over weeks 10–12, and candid low-production creative consistently outperforms brand-approved studio imagery. Teams evaluating TLA performance inside a standard 30-day sprint are systematically misreading the format.

## Why This Matters
- Demand gen directors allocating LinkedIn budget based on month-one unit economics may be cancelling their highest-compounding asset too early
- The creative insight (lo-fi outperforms professional by 45%) directly conflicts with standard brand governance processes, creating a structural tension between marketing ops and creative teams
- The CPC gap ($2.29 vs. $13.23) is so large it changes the math on LinkedIn as a performance channel, not just a brand channel—relevant for cybersecurity marketers with constrained CAC targets

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- G2's "Answer Economy" study (April 2026, 169 respondents): 51% of B2B software buyers now start their purchasing process in an AI chatbot—up from 29% twelve months prior. 69% said the chatbot led them to select a different vendor than originally planned; one-third purchased from a vendor they had never previously heard of. Implication: brand recall and top-of-funnel awareness built through traditional demand gen channels is increasingly insufficient for being included in AI-mediated purchasing shortlists.
Source: G2 / Learn G2 — https://learn.g2.com/tech-signals-demand-gen-playbook-2026

- 2X AI Visibility Index 2026: 96% of B2B companies are invisible during early AI-driven buyer discovery, appearing only in late-stage, highly specific queries. The average AI Presence Score across tracked B2B companies is 56.9/100, with 44% scoring below 50. Implication: most demand gen investment is reaching buyers who are already deep in evaluation, not at the awareness or consideration stage where AI now operates.
Source: Demand Gen Report — https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/2x-survey-finds-96-of-b2b-companies-are-invisible-in-ai-discovery/52536/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn launched global availability of Off-Platform Event Ads (full rollout May 6, 2026): Advertisers can now run in-feed event promotions that link directly to external registration pages, webinar platforms, or livestream destinations—without requiring a native LinkedIn Event Details Page. This removes a structural friction point for B2B marketers managing events on third-party platforms (e.g., Hopin, ON24, Zoom webinars). Attribution still flows through Campaign Manager, creating potential discrepancy with external registration platform data that teams will need to reconcile.
Source: Search Engine Land — https://searchengineland.com/linkedin-expands-event-ads-beyond-its-own-platform-475865
Source: Social Media Today — https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/linkedin-expands-off-platform-event-promotions/818759/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- ZenABM Thought Leader Ads Benchmark 2026 (211 B2B companies): Median CTR 2.68% at $2.29 median CPC—versus $13.23 CPC for single image ads and $15.61 for video ads. TLAs deliver 6.4x higher CTR than single image ads. CTR compounds over time: ~3.9% in week 1, rising to 8%+ by weeks 10–12, with meaningful drop-off not occurring until week 19. Candid, low-production imagery (conference selfies, whiteboard photos) outperforms studio photography by 45% in engagement on TLA format. Implication: the format is being evaluated on the wrong timeline and with the wrong creative assumptions by most teams.
Source: ZenABM — https://zenabm.com/blog/tla-benchmarks

MMM adoption in martech spend jumped from under 1% to 11% in one year. Only 21% of B2B marketers are confident in their attribution overall. Implication: the majority of B2B teams are optimizing budget on a measurement model that structurally underrepresents pipeline contribution.

"Customers are demanding outcomes, not effort." Implication: demand gen content and proof points for MSP-focused cybersecurity vendors need to shift from capability demonstration to performance evidence—case studies with measurable SLA data will outperform spec sheets.
