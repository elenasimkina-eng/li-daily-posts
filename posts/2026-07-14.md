## LinkedIn Post

The paid ads pullback in B2B is real. The question is whether the budget is moving to the right stage.

Kaylee Edmondson surveyed 50 mid-market B2B marketing leaders (DemandLoops) and found 72% actively cutting paid search, content syndication, or agency spend. Most of that budget is flowing toward events (46%) and partnerships (44%).

Both moves are defensible. But the buyer journey data complicates the logic.

G2's March 2026 research found 51% of B2B software buyers now begin research inside an AI chatbot before they open a search engine. Forrester found twice as many buyers name generative AI their most meaningful research source — ahead of vendor websites, analysts, and sales reps. In 6sense's study of nearly 4,000 buyers, 95% of the time the winning vendor was already on the shortlist before first outreach.

Events and partnerships are strong at conversion. They're weaker at shaping the shortlist formed before anyone registers.

Cutting paid because it's not producing qualified leads is a fair diagnosis. Redirecting that budget entirely toward later-stage channels while AI quietly resets how shortlists form is where the assumption deserves a harder look.

Wondering if anyone tracking self-reported attribution is seeing a divergence between when buyer awareness actually started and when the first trackable touch appeared.

## Central Insight
72% of B2B marketing leaders are cutting paid budgets and redirecting to events and partnerships — but buyer journey data shows shortlists are now formed in AI tools before anyone attends an event or connects through a partner. The pullback is a rational response to a wrong-stage diagnosis.

## Why This Matters
- Events and partnerships convert well, but reach buyers after AI-driven shortlists are already formed — redirecting budget there doesn't address the upstream discovery problem
- Demand gen directors in B2B cybersecurity face this exact tension: paid is underperforming, but the alternative channel choices may not address where buyer decisions actually originate
- Attribution systems capture only ~27% of the B2B buyer journey, so "paid isn't working" diagnoses may be based on incomplete visibility, not actual underperformance

- 51% of B2B software buyers now start research with an AI chatbot more often than Google (up from 29% in April 2025), per G2 March 2026 research. AI chatbots are now the #1 source influencing B2B software buyer shortlists, ahead of review sites, analyst firms, vendor websites, and sales reps.

- 69% of B2B buyers chose a different vendor than they initially planned because it appeared in a chatbot recommendation (G2 March 2026). Average shortlist has narrowed to 2.5 vendors.
Source: G2, referenced in buyer journey reports — https://company.g2.com/news/g2-innovations-activate-buyer-signals-and-build-pipeline
