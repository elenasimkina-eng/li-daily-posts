## LinkedIn Post

AI adoption in B2B marketing is at its highest point. The ability to prove what it does for pipeline isn't.

MarTech's 2026 analysis (martech.org/how-to-prove-roi-from-ai-workflow-integration-in-b2b-marketing/) tracked the share of B2B marketers who can demonstrate AI ROI: 49% last year, 41% this year, while deployment kept growing.

That divergence points to something structural. AI has become the default motion. Accountability for what it changes in the pipeline hasn't been built in alongside it.

Which means the budget conversation is running on adoption momentum, not evidence.

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- The share of B2B marketers who can demonstrate AI ROI dropped from 49% to 41% in a single year, despite AI deployment continuing to grow. High-maturity organizations are twice as likely to achieve solid ROI; 60% of teams that can prove AI ROI see 2x returns or better. The divergence signals a structural accountability problem, not a performance problem.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/how-to-prove-roi-from-ai-workflow-integration-in-b2b-marketing/

- 82% of B2B tech queries now trigger AI Overviews. The overlap between top-10 Google rankings and AI Overview citations collapsed from 75% (mid-2025) to 17–38% (early 2026). Brands cited in AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than non-cited brands for the same queries.
Source: Digital Applied — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-overviews-13-percent-queries-seo-traffic-impact
