## LinkedIn Post

Dreamdata's 2026 benchmark — covering 66 million sessions across 3.5 million customer journeys — finds the average B2B buyer journey now runs 272 days. The first 220 of those days are self-education: buyers forming a shortlist, building category understanding, and developing vendor preferences before any sales process touches them.

Separately, the 2X AI Innovation Lab analyzed 70 B2B companies and found 96% are invisible during early-stage AI-driven discovery. Only 4.3% appear when buyers ask the kind of exploratory, category-level questions that characterize those first seven months of research.

Taken together, the picture is uncomfortable.

Most B2B demand gen programs are calibrated to the last 52 days of a 272-day journey. MQL thresholds, intent signals, form fills, SDR sequences — these are instruments tuned to the window when buyers are already decided. The upstream portion, where the consideration set actually gets built, runs largely through AI chatbots and peer referrals that most vendors have no presence in.

51% of B2B software buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot rather than Google. If a company doesn't appear in those early conversations, it isn't being considered — not because the buyer rejected it, but because it was never surfaced.

The measurement problem compounds the structural one. Most pipeline dashboards track what happened after first contact. They have nothing to say about the 220 days before it. Which means teams don't see the gap — they see a funnel that appears to be working, against a universe of buyers who already chose someone else before arriving.

This starts looking less like a conversion problem and more like a presence problem.

## Topic Selection

## Recommended Topic
B2B buyers are seven months into self-directed AI research before demand gen programs ever touch them — and 96% of companies are invisible during that entire window.

## Selected Angle
Analytical

## Central Insight
The default demand gen model — activate at MQL, intercept at intent signal, convert at form fill — assumes the buyer journey starts where vendors enter it. Dreamdata's journey data (272 days total, 220 days of self-education) combined with AI visibility research (96% of B2B companies absent from early-stage AI discovery) reveals that most demand gen programs are competing for buyers whose opinions are already formed.

## Why This Matters
- Demand gen budgets and pipeline models are calibrated to the last 20% of the buyer journey, not the 80% where preferences are actually being shaped
- AI-driven early research is not a new channel to add — it is the environment where the consideration set gets built, often without vendor involvement
- Teams measuring MQL velocity and form fills are measuring outputs from a journey segment they are not influencing; the upstream part runs on AI, peer referrals, and third-party content most B2B companies do not appear in

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- DerivateX benchmarked 50 B2B SaaS companies across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini using 1,400 buyer-intent prompts. Average AI Presence Score: 56.9/100. 44% of companies scored below 50. The gap between highest (Clio, 89) and lowest (LeadSquared, 2) scorer was 87 points — both operating in established categories with active marketing teams. Implication: category leadership and ad investment do not predict AI discoverability.

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google AI Overviews now appear in 25%+ of searches (up from 13% 12 months ago). When present, organic CTR drops 61% for informational queries, and zero-click rates reach 80–83%. B2B technology sites face exposure rates around 70% for their typical query types. However, pages actually cited in AI Overviews see an average 18% CTR increase vs. traditional rankings. Implication: the channel bifurcates — irrelevance or elevated relevance, almost no middle ground.
Source: Search Engine Journal — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-cut-organic-clicks-38-field-study-finds/573145/

- 47% of AI Overview citations come from pages ranking below position 5 organically. Traditional SEO rank is not a reliable predictor of AI citation inclusion. Strong rankings and strong AI visibility are measured by different systems.
Source: Position.digital — https://www.position.digital/blog/ai-seo-statistics/
