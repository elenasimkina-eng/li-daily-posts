## LinkedIn Post

Most teams report their AI adoption rate. Almost none report where in the funnel that AI is actually doing anything.

87% of B2B marketers used AI in at least one GTM workflow this year. That's the headline from Growth Unhinged's 2026 State of AI for B2B GTM report. The number underneath it: roughly 6% of organizations qualify as high performers actually extracting bottom-line value from it.

The distribution explains the gap. G2's 2026 AI in B2B Marketing research found 91% of teams use AI for content creation. 31% for lead scoring. 25% for personalization.

AI landed first where it was cheapest to deploy and easiest to demonstrate — the production layer. The decision layer, where AI would actually change conversion rates, is still largely manual.

That's the trade-off almost nobody names: deploying AI at scale across content production generates efficiency metrics, not pipeline metrics.

The downstream signal: the share of B2B marketers who can demonstrate AI ROI fell from roughly 50% to 41% year over year. A deployment problem appearing as a measurement problem.

For demand gen teams, adoption rate has stopped being the relevant benchmark. Funnel position is.

## Central Insight
Most teams report aggregate AI adoption numbers, but the data reveals AI has landed almost exclusively at the content production layer — where it creates efficiency without meaningful pipeline lift — while the decision layer (scoring, routing, personalization) remains largely manual. The 87% adoption rate obscures a 6% high-performance rate, and the gap explains itself once you see where in the funnel AI is actually deployed.

## Why This Matters
- Demand gen directors are being asked to demonstrate AI ROI, but most investments are concentrated where AI is easiest to measure (content output) rather than where it changes financial outcomes (conversion rates)
- The year-over-year decline in marketers who can demonstrate AI ROI (from ~50% to 41%) signals the current deployment pattern is producing diminishing returns
- For cybersecurity/MSP demand gen teams managing long, complex sales cycles with 8–10 stakeholder buying committees, the decision layer (account scoring, intent signal activation, personalization) is where AI would actually shorten the buying journey

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- 96% of B2B marketers report using AI, but only 26% rate their team's execution as high. 91% deploy AI for content generation; only 31% for lead scoring and 25% for personalization. The share who can demonstrate AI ROI has fallen from ~50% to 41% year over year — widening the adoption-execution gap rather than closing it.
Source: G2 AI in B2B Marketing 2026 — https://learn.g2.com/ai-in-b2b-marketing

- The 2026 State of AI for B2B GTM (Growth Unhinged) finds 87% of marketers used generative AI in at least one workflow, yet only ~6% of organizations qualify as high performers actually extracting bottom-line value. AI is being deployed as a production accelerator, not a decision engine.
Source: Growth Unhinged — https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2026-state-of-ai-gtm-report

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn's 360Brew algorithm — a 150-billion-parameter model replacing five separate content retrieval systems — is fully deployed. It reads post intent, not keywords, and prioritizes "knowledge exchange" signals over raw engagement. LinkedIn now commands 41% of total B2B ad budgets. ROAS: LinkedIn 121% vs. Google Search 67% vs. Meta 51%. However, non-branded Google search budgets dropped from 37% to 33% while CPCs on non-branded terms are up 29%.
Source: Dreamdata 2026 LinkedIn Ads B2B Benchmarks — https://dreamdata.io/linkedin-ads-b2b-benchmarks | ALM Corp — https://almcorp.com/blog/linkedin-ads-ultimate-guide-2026/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- 2026 ABM Benchmark Survey (Demand Gen Report): MQL→SQL median is 13%; top decile is 31%. The 18-point gap is almost entirely explained by AI-assisted predictive scoring adoption. Pipeline coverage median is 3.2× quota; top quartile at 4.8×. Programs below 2.5× coverage are in leading-indicator distress — missed quota follows 1–2 quarters later.
Source: Demand Gen Report ABM Benchmark Survey 2026 — https://www.demandgenreport.com/resources/read-the-2026-account-based-marketing-benchmark-survey-results/53096/

- 68% of B2B marketers are increasing lead volume YoY; only 32% say lead quality improved proportionally. 91% use intent data but only 24% report exceptional ROI — signals are collected but not activated.
Source: Digital Applied Demand Generation Statistics 2026 — https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/demand-generation-statistics-2026-pipeline-data

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Semrush data: 93% of Google AI Mode searches end without a click. Zero-click now 58.5% of all US Google searches (77% on mobile). B2B tech queries trigger AI Overviews 82% of the time. Organic CTR drops 34.5–58% when an AI Overview is present. Overlap between top 10 organic rankings and AI citations has collapsed to 17–38%; BrightEdge reports 400% more citations pulled from positions 21–30.
Source: Omnibound AI SEO Statistics 2026 — https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/ai-seo-statistics | Pasquale Pillitteri — https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/811/google-ai-mode-zero-click-seo-2026-en

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- The operational reality behind AI-assisted GTM: AI has been deployed primarily at the production layer (content generation: 91%) and rarely at the decision layer (lead scoring: 31%). Result: efficiency gains at the top of funnel, marginal pipeline impact. Enterprise companies are now spending ~$14,000 per employee on hallucination-protection governance — a cost almost never factored into AI content ROI calculations.
Source: Digiday — https://digiday.com/marketing/marketers-are-keen-to-use-generative-ai-in-ad-campaigns-but-hidden-costs-lurk/

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Cybersecurity buying committees have grown from 4–6 stakeholders (2020) to 6–10 (2026), averaging 8+. When an MSP recommends a vendor, sales cycles are 50–70% shorter and close rates 2–3× higher than direct. Yet 77% of MSPs still cite lack of client urgency as their top sales challenge — the channel advantage is structural; the activation problem remains.
Source: Callbox Inc MSP Cybersecurity Sales Strategies 2026 — https://www.callboxinc.com/growth-hacking/msp-cybersecurity-sales-strategies/
