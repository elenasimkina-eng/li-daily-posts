## LinkedIn Post

Sixty-nine percent of B2B buyers chose a different vendor than they planned. One in three bought from a company they'd never heard of before the AI conversation started.

That data, from G2's March 2026 "Answer Economy" survey of 1,076 buyers, sits at the center of @Constantine von Hoffman's MarTech piece on AI shortlists as the new B2B battleground. His argument holds up. But the research has a second layer.

Forrester's 2026 Buyers' Journey survey of 18,000 buyers found generative AI is now the most meaningful research source by a wide margin, cited by 2x more respondents than any other source. Vendor websites, peer experts, and sales reps all rank below it.

The deal forms before the vendor knows there's a deal.

What the "win the AI shortlist" framing underweights is what happens immediately after. Buyers cross-validate. G2's qualitative work with 39 B2B software marketers found that cross-chatbot consistency, a vendor described the same way across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, is now the top trust signal. When it's inconsistent, the shortlist position erodes before the first sales call.

Getting into AI's consideration set is the opening. Credibility across every validation surface is what keeps you there.

Curious whether others tracking AI-sourced pipeline are seeing the same divergence between initial AI citations and actual conversion.

## Central Insight
AI chatbots have become the dominant first-filter in B2B vendor selection, redistributing deals to previously unknown companies — but buyers immediately cross-validate AI-generated shortlists across G2, peer networks, and analyst sources. Getting cited by AI earns consideration; consistent credibility across every validation surface is what keeps you on the list.

## Why This Matters
- Most demand gen teams are currently concentrating investment on AI discoverability (AEO, GEO, content for LLMs) while underinvesting in the cross-channel credibility infrastructure that determines whether AI-generated consideration converts
- The 1-in-3 buyers who purchased from a previously unknown vendor is a structural opportunity for challengers — but only if their validation surfaces match their AI presence
- In cybersecurity specifically, trust is a primary purchase driver, making the AI-to-validation gap particularly costly when it exists

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- G2's "Answer Economy" report (1,076 B2B buyers surveyed, March 2026) found 51% now start software research in an AI chatbot — up from 29% one year prior. 69% chose a different vendor than initially planned based on AI guidance; one in three bought from a company they had never heard of before the conversation. AI is not just augmenting discovery — it is redistributing deals outside the existing consideration set.
Source: G2 — https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report

- Forrester's 2026 State of Business Buying (18,000 global buyers) found generative AI and conversational search are now the single most meaningful research source — cited by 2x more buyers than any other source, outranking vendor websites, peer experts, and sales reps. 94% of B2B buyers now use AI at some stage of the purchase, up from 89% in 2025.
Source: Forrester — https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- LinkedIn organic reach has dropped ~50% year-over-year. Company pages receive approximately 5% of user feed allocation; personal profiles dominate at ~65%. External links reduce reach by ~60%. Dwell time is now the #1 ranking signal. The B2B assumption that company page content drives awareness and demand is no longer supportable by the distribution math.
Source: ZoomSphere — https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm-2026-why-generic-ai-content-kills-your-organic-reach

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- B2B buyers now spend approximately 5 hours in AI-assisted research for every 1 hour with a sales rep. The majority of that research occurs inside AI tools — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode — with no trackable signal generated. By the time a buyer submits a form, the shortlist is already formed.
Source: NatLaw Review — https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/b2b-buying-statistics-2026-buyers-spend-5-hours-ai-search-1-hour-sales

- Despite AI accelerating individual research speed, 86% of B2B purchases still stall (Forrester 2026). The average purchase now involves 13 internal stakeholders and 9 external participants. Stalling is driven by internal alignment failure, not information gaps. AI is compressing discovery but leaving consensus-building untouched.
Source: Forrester — https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Zero-click searches reached 68% of all Google queries in June 2026 (SparkToro study). B2B tech and services sit in the highest-impact zone. AI Overviews appear on 48% of all Google searches globally (BrightEdge). Vendors gain share of mind without generating any traffic attribution signal.
Source: Relevant Audience — https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/google-zero-click-hit-68-percent-in-2026/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- Only 4.3% of companies appear in early-stage AI buyer questions — the stage where shortlists are assembled. The remaining 95.7% appear primarily in queries from buyers who already know the company name. Most brands are winning recognition contests, not discovery competitions.
Source: 2X AI Visibility Index — https://2x.marketing/b2b-ai-visibility-by-growth-stage-report/

- Hybrid attribution models (multi-touch + marketing mix modeling, reconciled by AI) predict revenue with 22 percentage points more accuracy than last-touch. Teams running attribution-capable models report 1.6x larger marketing-sourced pipeline.
Source: Improvado — https://improvado.io/blog/b2b-marketing-attribution

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- Cybersecurity is the fastest-growing MSP service segment at 18% annual growth vs. 14% for the overall MSP market. It is both the most-delivered service (55% of MSPs) and top expansion priority (41%). Vereigen Media's Cybersecurity Marketing Spend Benchmark 2026 identifies trust, brand authority, and verified engagement — not lead volume — as primary drivers of revenue performance.
Source: Vereigen Media — https://vereigenmedia.com/cybersecurity-marketing-spend-benchmark-report/
