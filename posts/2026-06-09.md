## LinkedIn Post

Lee Odden's finding holds up. Original research remains the most trusted B2B thought leadership format — 93% of practitioners call it effective for engagement and leads, according to the State of B2B Thought Leadership 2026 (Ascend2 / TopRank Marketing).

But there's one assumption underneath it that is starting to fracture.

94% of B2B buyers now use LLMs to research vendors before any direct engagement. That's 80 to 100 million B2B research prompts daily across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Not a marginal behavior.

And LLMs don't cite sources the way Google ranks them.

Cross-platform citation density matters more than keyword relevance. Brands cited across four or more platforms are 2.8x more likely to appear in ChatGPT responses. LinkedIn is the second most cited domain by LLMs, appearing in 11% of AI-generated responses.

The research format is right. The distribution logic underneath most thought leadership programs is not.

Producing original research and publishing it to a blog is half the equation. Getting it distributed across enough citation surfaces is the part most programs haven't caught up to.

Curious whether anyone tracking thought leadership performance is seeing the same divergence between AI citation rates and traditional search outcomes.

## Central Insight
Original research is still the right thought leadership format, but the assumption about how it gets discovered is quietly breaking: buyers now research via LLMs, and LLMs surface sources through cross-platform citation density, not the SEO and social channels where most B2B thought leadership programs distribute their work.

- 94% of B2B buyers use LLMs to research vendors before contact, meaning vendor perception is now formed in channels most thought leadership programs don't optimize for

- LinkedIn is now the second most LLM-cited domain (11% of AI responses), yet most thought leadership programs treat it as a secondary distribution channel, not a citation-building one

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- 94% of B2B buyers now use LLMs to research vendors before any direct vendor engagement, generating 80–100M B2B research prompts daily across ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, and Gemini. Despite AI-mediated research, buyers still complete 16 vendor interactions per deal (virtually unchanged from 17 in 2024). Implication: AI is reshaping how vendor perception is formed pre-engagement, but has not shortened the deal engagement cycle.
Source: Machine Relations AI — https://machinerelations.ai/research/b2b-ai-vendor-research-2026

- AI ROI confidence fell from ~50% to 41% among B2B marketers in 2026, as leadership now demands clear pipeline linkage rather than efficiency claims. 72% report AI-generated content hurt brand distinctiveness.

Prospecting Agent moved to $1/qualified lead, with 57% QoQ activation increase and 10% higher close rates across 10,000+ customers. Implication: outcome-based pricing for AI tools is becoming default — CFOs will now compare agent costs directly to BDR costs.
Source: CMSWire — https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/hubspot-shifts-breeze-ai-agents-to-pay-per-result-pricing/
