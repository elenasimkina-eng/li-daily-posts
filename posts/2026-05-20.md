## LinkedIn Post

Search traffic is declining. Demo requests are growing. That combination used to be a red flag — a funnel broken at the middle. In 2026, it may be the opposite signal.

Factors.ai's B2B benchmark data shows paid search conversion rates are down 20% at the median. Yet the same companies are seeing demo requests up 17.4%. Companies where organic traffic dropped by 28% still saw conversion rates grow 18%. (https://www.factors.ai/blog/b2b-demos-increasing-while-search-conversions-drop)

The explanation is structural, not tactical. Buyers are completing more of their consideration journey inside AI conversations before they ever arrive at a vendor's site. WebFX tracked 2.3 billion sessions and found AI-referred conversions grew 6,432% year-over-year — while AI still accounts for 0.18% of total sessions. The volume is small. The intent density is not. (https://www.webfx.com/blog/seo/gen-ai-search-trends/)

Similarweb's 2026 GenAI Brand Visibility Index puts a number on what that looks like in practice: ChatGPT-referred visitors spend 15 minutes on-site versus 8 from Google, generate 12 pageviews versus 9, and convert at 7% versus 5%. They arrive already informed, already pre-qualifying. (https://www.similarweb.com/corp/reports/the-2026-generative-ai-brand-visibility-index/)

What's interesting is what this means for how demand gen teams measure themselves. If AI is doing the consideration-stage work that paid search used to do — and doing it invisibly, inside conversations that don't show up in your attribution model — then declining click volume starts to look less like a campaign problem and more like a measurement problem.

The pipeline is intact. The discovery layer has moved somewhere teams aren't looking.

## Recommended Topic
The demand generation paradox: paid search conversions are falling while demo requests are rising, because AI is doing the consideration-stage qualification work before buyers reach vendor sites.

## Selected Angle
Analytical

## Central Insight
The consideration stage of the B2B buyer journey has migrated into AI chat interfaces that are invisible to standard demand gen attribution. Teams measuring search performance as a proxy for pipeline health are reading a signal that no longer describes where qualification is actually happening.

## Why This Matters
- Demand gen teams optimizing to recover declining search metrics may be solving the wrong problem — the funnel isn't broken, the measurement layer is misaligned with where buyers now operate
- Demo requests rising while traffic falls is a structural signal, not a contradiction: it means buyers arrive later in the journey, more pre-qualified, having already narrowed their shortlist in AI conversations
- For cybersecurity vendors targeting MSPs, this is especially acute — AI-assisted vendor shortlisting means that brands absent from AI citation ecosystems are being eliminated before the first sales conversation

## Research Brief

---

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- AI referral traffic grew 796% year-over-year across 2.3 billion site sessions tracked by WebFX (Jan 2024–Dec 2025); conversions from AI-referred visitors grew 6,432% YoY. AI still represents only 0.18% of total web sessions, but arrival intent is materially higher — visitors typically arrive at pricing, technical review, or credibility-assessment pages. Teams measuring AI performance by session volume will miss the structural shift entirely.
Source: WebFX — https://www.webfx.com/blog/seo/gen-ai-search-trends/

- 18.7% of publishers cited in AI-generated search results are directly influenceable by brands through affiliate and content partnerships, per LQ Digital's Q1 2026 analysis of 100,000+ AI-generated responses across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Google AI Overviews has the highest share of influenceable citations among major AI platforms. This represents an actionable gap: brands with existing affiliate infrastructure already have the relationships needed to expand AI citation presence.
Source: PR.com (LQ Digital) — https://www.pr.com/press-release/965244

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Apollo.io launched its app in ChatGPT on April 29, 2026, powered by MCP (Model Context Protocol), enabling full outbound GTM workflows — prospecting, record enrichment, sequencing, and pipeline analysis — inside a single ChatGPT conversation. This continues the pattern of GTM platforms competing to become the default AI-native execution interface rather than a SaaS dashboard accessed separately.
Source: PR Newswire (Apollo.io) — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/apolloio-launches-app-in-chatgpt-302756434.html

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- Factors.ai B2B benchmark data: paid search conversion rates are down 20% at the median. Demo requests are up 9.5% overall; the median company saw demo request growth of 17.4%. Among companies where organic traffic declined 28%, conversion rates still grew 18%. The pattern points to a structural shift: buyers are finishing more of the research and consideration journey in AI tools before arriving at vendor sites, arriving later and more pre-qualified.
Source: Factors.ai — https://www.factors.ai/blog/b2b-demos-increasing-while-search-conversions-drop

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Similarweb's 2026 GenAI Brand Visibility Index (tracking 113 brands across ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Perplexity, US data, January 2026): 35% of US consumers now use AI at the product discovery stage versus 13.6% using traditional search. ChatGPT-referred visitors spend 15 minutes on-site versus 8 minutes from Google, generate 12 pageviews per visit versus 9, and convert to transactional actions at 7% versus 5%. Structured authority, topic depth, and third-party citation presence are the primary drivers of AI visibility, not keyword rankings.
Source: Similarweb — https://www.similarweb.com/corp/reports/the-2026-generative-ai-brand-visibility-index/

- Column Five Media analysis: 90% of higher-intent B2B buyers clicked through to at least one AI-cited source when encountering Google AI Overviews during research. Forrester estimates AI-generated traffic is currently 2–6% of B2B organic traffic and growing at 40%+ month-over-month. The strategic implication: citation presence in AI Overviews is generating click-through from the highest-intent segment of the research audience.
Source: Column Five Media — https://www.columnfivemedia.com/ai-search-visibility-stats-that-might-surprise-you-in-2026/

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- The Apollo.io + ChatGPT MCP launch is part of a broader agentic GTM workflow trend: Outreach (Omni), Highspot (GTM Agent), and Apollo are all embedding execution capabilities into AI conversation interfaces. This signals the next platform consolidation moment — GTM teams may route more workflow through AI chat layers than dedicated SaaS tools within 12–18 months.
Source: PR Newswire (Apollo.io) — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/apolloio-launches-app-in-chatgpt-302756434.html

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- 71% of MSPs reported year-over-year revenue growth in cybersecurity — the highest of any service category. Cybersecurity is both the most-delivered MSP service (55%) and the top priority for MSP expansion (41%). The managed security market is projected to grow from $93B in 2025 to $106B in 2026, a 14.4% increase.
Source: ScalePad 2026 MSP Trends Report — https://www.scalepad.com/blog/2026-msp-trends-report-launch/

- 48% of MSPs rank AI and automation as the top client need for 2026 — ahead of security and backup — yet only 13% currently generate meaningful revenue from AI services. This gap between client AI demand and MSP AI delivery creates a vendor positioning window for cybersecurity vendors that can speak to AI-adjacent security risks in MSP language.
Source: Gitnux AI in MSP Industry Statistics 2026 — https://gitnux.org/ai-in-the-msp-industry-statistics/

- ChannelPro published a special report on cybersecurity resources for MSPs (May 19, 2026), covering the growing mismatch between how cybersecurity vendors market their products and how MSPs actually evaluate and recommend vendor solutions to clients.
