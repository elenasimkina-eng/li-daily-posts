## LinkedIn Post

Brand awareness used to guarantee consideration. That assumption is breaking.

According to Wynter's 2026 B2B CMO Buyer Journey Report, 84% of CMOs now use AI tools for vendor discovery — and 68% of them start there before opening Google. The shortlist is often formed inside ChatGPT or Perplexity before a buyer ever visits your website, reads a review, or clicks an ad.

What's striking is what happens next. A multi-source analysis of 680 million citations found that 69% of B2B buyers chose a different vendor than they'd originally planned based on AI guidance, and one in three purchased from a brand they had never heard of before. AI recommendations are now the single most influential source for vendor shortlists at 17.1% — ahead of review sites, vendor websites, and peer referrals.

The harder number: 22% of buyers excluded a vendor from consideration specifically because it didn't appear in AI-generated recommendations — even when they were already aware of that brand.

Which means the funnel isn't just getting shorter. The entry point has moved upstream, into a layer most demand generation teams aren't measuring or optimizing for.

This starts looking less like awareness spend and more like citation strategy. The question isn't just "can buyers find us on Google?" It's "what does an AI say when a buyer asks who solves this problem?"

The conversion data reinforces the stakes. AI search traffic converts at 14.2% versus Google organic's 2.8%, according to Exposure Ninja's 2026 analysis. The discovery channel with the least coverage in most marketing plans is producing the highest-quality traffic.

Source: Wynter 2026 B2B CMO Buyer Journey Report via MarTech (https://martech.org/the-new-b2b-battleground-is-getting-on-ais-shortlist/) and multi-source citation analysis via PR Newswire (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/73-of-b2b-buyers-use-ai-tools-in-purchase-research-multi-source-analysis-finds-302733319.html)

## Research Brief

**Key B2B Marketing & Demand Gen Updates (Last 24 Hours)**

**AI in B2B Marketing & AI Visibility**
- 84% of CMOs now use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) for vendor discovery, up from 24% a year ago, and 68% of those CMOs begin their vendor research in AI before opening Google — meaning the shortlist is often formed before any traditional search begins. For demand gen, this means brand presence in AI outputs is no longer optional.
Source: Wynter 2026 B2B CMO Buyer Journey Report, via MarTech — https://martech.org/the-new-b2b-battleground-is-getting-on-ais-shortlist/

- 69% of B2B buyers chose a different software vendor than initially planned based on AI chatbot guidance; one-third purchased from a vendor they had never previously heard of. AI-generated recommendations now outrank all other discovery sources at 17.1% influence, ahead of review sites (15.1%), vendor websites (12.8%), and peer referrals (8.9%).
Source: Multi-source analysis of 680M citations, reported via PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/73-of-b2b-buyers-use-ai-tools-in-purchase-research-multi-source-analysis-finds-302733319.html

- AI search traffic converts at 14.2% vs. Google organic's 2.8%, according to Exposure Ninja's 2026 analysis; Claude users convert at 16.8%, ChatGPT at 14.2%, Perplexity at 12.4%. The conversion advantage is not marginal — it is 5x.
Source: Omnibound, citing Exposure Ninja — https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/b2b-buying-statistics

**Platform & Algorithm Changes**
- Google's May 2026 Core Update launched alongside the I/O AI Search Overhaul, combining a traditional quality signal update with the expanded rollout of AI Mode. Top-10 rankers now account for only ~38% of AI Overview citations, down from 76% in mid-2025 — high rankings no longer reliably predict AI visibility.
Source: Search Engine Journal — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/seo-pulse-google-launches-core-update-amid-i-o-ai-search-overhaul/575676/

- HubSpot moved Breeze AI agents to outcome-based pricing in April 2026: Customer Agent at $0.50/resolved conversation (down from $1.00/conversation); Prospecting Agent at $1/lead. The Customer Agent resolves 65% of conversations; Prospecting Agent shows 57% QoQ activation growth. This pricing model shift is spreading — Salesforce, Zendesk, and Intercom have all moved in the same direction.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/hubspot-moves-to-outcome-based-pricing-for-some-breeze-ai-agents/

**ABM & Demand Generation**
- Buying committees for deals over $50K now average 11.2 stakeholders, up from 9.7 in 2024, and average sales cycles have lengthened to 121 days (mid-market) and 218 days (enterprise). For AI-related purchases, Forrester estimates buying groups expand to 20+ participants.
Source: Corporate Visions / Forrester — https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends/

- AI-generated dynamic content adoption in ABM stacks rose from 11% in Q1 2024 to 38% in Q1 2026 — the fastest-growing category on the ABM stack. Multichannel ABM engagement improves by 72% when coordinated across email, LinkedIn, phone, and advertising.
Source: ABM Agency / Demandbase — https://abmagency.com/the-state-of-abm-data-in-2026-intent-signals-and-ai-powered-insights/

**SEO / Organic / AI Search**
- Zero-click searches now account for approximately 58–69% of all US Google searches depending on the data source (SparkToro/Similarweb); Bain & Company puts the figure at ~60%. B2B publishers whose informational content gets synthesized inside AI agent responses without generating a click face structural referral loss.
Source: MarTech / Bain — https://martech.org/ai-is-rewriting-visibility-in-the-zero-click-search-era/

- The overlap between top Google results and AI-cited sources has dropped from 70% to below 20%, per Brandlight's research cited across multiple GEO studies. Citations inside AI Overviews now drive 35% more organic clicks than a standard position-one result — being cited has become more valuable than ranking below the overview.
Source: LaunchCodex / Discovered Labs — https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/google-ai-mode-may-2026-search-update

**Martech / GTM / Market Signals**
- Salesforce's Agentforce Operations launched to automate back-office marketing workflows, extending AI agent capabilities from customer-facing to operational functions. The broader industry pattern: the platforms that win enterprise budget are converging on agentic workflows, not point AI features.
Source: MarTech — https://martech.org/salesforce-launches-agentforce-operations-to-automate-back-office-work/

- 22% of B2B buyers have excluded a vendor from consideration because it did not appear in AI-generated recommendations — even when they were already aware of that brand. Half of buyers reported seriously evaluating or purchasing a vendor discovered exclusively through AI with no prior brand awareness.
Source: AuthorityTech / Wynter — https://authoritytech.io/curated/ai-search-experiments-buying-decisions-cmo-moves-2026

**Cybersecurity / MSP Signals**
- 91% of SMBs fear AI-driven attacks; 47% will pay more for 24/7 monitoring and faster response; 44% will pay more for AI-driven detection and response — establishing AI capability as a price-premium driver in the MSP security conversation.
Source: WatchGuard MSP Survey 2026 — https://www.watchguard.com/wgrd-resource-center/msp-survey-2026

- MSPs report 77% of clients cite "lack of urgency" as the top sales challenge. Yet cybersecurity MSP services are growing at 18% annually, outpacing overall MSP market growth at 14%. The gap between purchase intent difficulty and market growth suggests the category's tailwind is structural, not demand-driven.
Source: ChannelE2E / Cybersecurity Dive — https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/msps-need-ai-to-fight-ai-fueled-cyberthreats-guardz/820371/
